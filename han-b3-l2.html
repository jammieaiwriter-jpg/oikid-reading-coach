全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Today is Club Contest Day at our school. Every club shows its own project, and many guests visit our classrooms. My science teacher does not teach a normal lesson today — she takes our whole class around the school instead.

In the gym, the robot club shows a small robot. An engineer from a local company is a guest judge today. He tells us the robot's design is very smart, and he asks who built it.

While the engineer talks with the robot club, I hear music from the next room. The violin club is playing before the judges arrive. Their teacher, Ms. Wang, says today's lesson is different — no writing, just watching and listening.

When the violin song ends, everyone claps. Then a magic show starts in the same room. The magician shows us a card trick, and it looks like real magic!

Next, I walk to the PE room to see the swim club's video. Their coach shows an underwater video of a swimmer named Kevin. He swims fast, and he can hold his breath for a long time.

In the video, Kevin talks about himself. He says swimming looks difficult at first, but it is good for his health, so he practices every day after school.

Outside, the soccer club shows an interesting poster about their team's history. The poster tells us their team was very poor at the start, but the players did not give up, and now they win many games.

Just before the contest ends, I hear my own name — I am one of the photo club's guests! I do not know this at first, but Ms. Chen's camera club takes pictures of the whole day.

"Whose photo is this?" Ms. Chen asks with a smile. It is mine, and I look at myself on the wall.

At the end of the day, our principal calls us back to the gym to announce the winner of Club Contest Day. She thinks every club did a great job, and she offers free snacks to every student before we go home.

I finish my homework early tonight because I want to write about this fun festival. I learn that trying something new is never wrong. My favorite subject is math, but even Chinese class feels exciting after a day like this!

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：從屬連接詞 when/while/before/as soon as/until（＝知洞 GP-33）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）

區塊 6 / 6　⑥ GEPT 延伸閱讀：New Glasses for the Ball Game講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍，換一篇鈕輪替 4 篇：眼鏡行配眼鏡／相館洗照片／腳踏車行補胎／水電行叔叔）
【挑戰閱讀】約 200–300 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。換一篇鈕會輪替另外三篇（相館洗照片、腳踏車行補胎、水電行叔叔）。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Last week, I did not see the soccer ball well during practice. My coach talked to my mom about it. On Saturday, my mom took me to the glasses shop near our house.

The shop owner was a kind old man. He asked me to sit down and look at a big board on the wall. The letters were big at the top and small at the bottom, in many rows.

I read the big letters at the top with no problem. But when I got to the small letters at the bottom, I did not read them well. The man put some numbers on a piece of paper.

Then I needed to choose new glasses. There were many frames on the shelf. Some were big, some were small, some were round, and some were square. I tried five or six pairs in front of the mirror.

My mom liked a black pair, but I liked a blue pair. The blue pair was light, and it fit my face well. I asked the man, "How much does this pair cost?" He said the price to my mom.

The man said it takes about one hour to make new glasses, so we walked to a small shop nearby and bought some snacks. When we came back, my glasses were ready on the table.

I put on my new glasses right away. I looked out the window, and I saw every leaf on the tree. Even the small words on a sign across the street looked clear.

The next day, I put on my new glasses and went to soccer practice. I saw the ball very well, and I did not miss it. My coach said I looked like a real player.
