區塊 12 / 14　Grammar Corner講義 p14
一般直接加 ing；子音+母音+子音要重複子音；子音+e 結尾要先刪 e 再加 ing。
認字（先聽，再唸對才算過）
單字
ride　騎
逐句唸過
唸 1
Many verbs in the present progressive end with the letters ing.
唸 2
Play and ing makes playing.
唸 3
Draw and ing makes drawing.
唸 4
Double the last consonant and add ing when the verb ends in consonant, vowel, consonant.
唸 5
Dig and ing makes digging.
唸 6
Add ing after deleting e when the verb ends in consonant plus e.
唸 7
Type and ing makes typing.
唸 8
Write and ing makes writing.
唸 9
Ride and ing makes riding.
唸 10
Bake and ing makes baking.
練習（講義原題）
第 1 題
paint + ing = ______
