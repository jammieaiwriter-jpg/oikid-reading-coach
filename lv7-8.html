2. Vocabulary Preview
crust
地殼、外殼
The Earth’s crust is constantly moving.
constantly
持續地
The world is constantly changing.
fault
斷層
A fault is a crack in the Earth’s surface.
occurrence
發生的事
An earthquake is a natural occurrence.
vibration
震動
Vibrations travel through the crust.
seismograph
地震儀
A seismograph records earthquake motions.
magnitude
規模、大小
Magnitude refers to the size of an earthquake.
tsunami
海嘯
An underwater earthquake can trigger a tsunami.
coastline
海岸線
A tsunami can overwhelm coastlines.
volcano
火山
A volcano may become active after an earthquake.
erupt
噴發
Volcanoes can erupt due to earthquakes.
3. Key Sentence Shadowing
Sentence 1
The Earth’s crust is constantly moving around the globe.
Sentence 2
When the crust moves, it causes blocks of the earth to slip past one another.
Sentence 3
A seismograph is a device that measures and records the motions of a crust.
Sentence 4
The magnitude of an earthquake refers to its size.
Sentence 5
Earthquakes may happen underwater as well, which results in tsunamis.
Sentence 6
Sometimes, volcanoes may also become active and erupt due to earthquakes.
