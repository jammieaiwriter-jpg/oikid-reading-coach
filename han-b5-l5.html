區塊 1 / 8　① 課文朗讀：A Day at the Comic Book Convention（原創）講義 原創課次對應文本（漫畫展手作 cosplay 情境）
原創課次文本，用本課單字和文法（GP-56 基礎關係代名詞 who/which/that、GP-32 地方與時間副詞片語）寫成（課本課文請看課本跟著念）。一次念一整段，念完做理解題——題目從這篇文字就能作答。
全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Comic books and anime are my biggest hobby, so I feel excited every time a comic book convention comes to our city. This year, the event takes place in the public square downtown, and it starts early in the morning.

For weeks, I work on a costume that has a fox tail and a small bow behind one ear. My mom, who is good with a needle, helps me pin round dots onto a string of tiny pearls.

On the night before the convention, I take a quick bath and lay my costume at the bottom of my bed so I will not forget it. Early the next morning, I arrive at the square before most people get there.

Rows of booths line both sides of the square. In one booth, a friendly businessman who sells handmade pins waves and calls out to visitors passing by. Next to him, another booth sells colorful stickers of every planet.

Near the middle, an artist who draws anime characters gets a lot of attention. People stop and ask her to describe how she creates a character, and a magazine later writes an article about the main topic of her drawings.

At noon, a group of students performs a short play based on a famous anime series, and everyone claps loudly when it ends.

A tall cosplayer, whose costume looks just like the character from my favorite show, walks by with a camera hanging around his neck. He will join a costume contest, and the best costume will win a prize.

I notice my neighbor, who also loves anime, standing near a booth that sells comic books. We hug and start talking about the new show that will appear on TV next month.

A foreigner walks up and asks what the word cosplayer means. My neighbor takes out a small dictionary and shows him that the noun belongs to a new kind of hobby.

My neighbor studies at a college nearby, and she still carries an English workbook to do exercise between classes. Her knowledge of words helps her lead the foreigner around the event.

Near the restroom, I see a staff member in a uniform who was born in this city and works for the convention business every year.

By the end of the day, my feet are tired, but my smile does not disappear. This convention, which brings together anime fans, artists, and cosplayers, always reminds me of the true meaning of this hobby.

Every autumn, our school holds a book fair in the gym, and students look forward to it all year. This year, the fair opens on a Monday morning and lasts for five days.

Long tables are set up in rows, and each table has a different topic: story books, comic books, science books, and English dictionaries for beginners.

My class visits the fair together on the first day. Our teacher tells us that we can buy up to three books with the money our parents gave us.

I want to buy a book that explains how planets move around the sun. My best friend wants a comic book about a brave young artist who saves her village.

At one table, a helper shows younger students how to find easy books. She smiles and explains that reading a little every day builds strong knowledge over time.

Near the end of the fair, the school gives a small prize to the class that reads the most books. Everyone works hard to win, but nobody feels too worried about it.

On the last afternoon, students who finish reading three books can join a short quiz for extra points. I answer every question correctly and feel very proud of myself.

By the end of the week, our library shelves are full of new stories. I already look forward to next year's book fair, when new topics and new books will arrive.
