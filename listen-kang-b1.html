A Letter to My New Pen Pal

信件
📋 先看題目（聽完再作答）
  1. Who is writing this letter?
  2. What is Leo's mother's job?
  3. What is the name of Leo's pet dog?
語速：
分段聽：

Dear Nora,

Hi! My name is Leo. I am a junior high school student. I am really happy to have a pen pal from your class!

Let me tell you about my family. My family is not big, but we are warm and close. My father is a farmer, and my mother is a cook at our school.

My father is strict, but he is also kind. My mother is beautiful and very nice. They are the same age, and they are both really busy.

I have one sister and one brother. My sister is young and very cute. My brother is in senior high school. He is tall and handsome.

We also have a pet dog. Its name is Coco. Coco is a good pet, and it is friendly to our neighbors, too.

My aunt and uncle live near our house. Their daughter is my cousin, and she is my best friend. We are in the same class this year!

Is your family big or small? Are you a farmer's child, too? Please write back and tell me about your family. Do you want to send me a photo of your family?

Your new friend,

Leo

Ben's family is a good example. His father is a doctor, and his mother is a nurse. They are both busy every day. Ben is their only child. He does not have a brother or a sister, but he has a cousin near his house.

Many children today are like Ben. They live with just their parents, but they are still close to their grandparents, aunts, and uncles. Family is not about the number of people. It is about being warm and kind to each other.

Small or big, every family is special in its own way.

分段聽：

Do you know your Chinese zodiac animal? In Chinese culture, there are twelve zodiac animals. This is a very old and famous tradition.

The twelve animals are the rat, the ox, the tiger, the rabbit, the dragon, the snake, the horse, the goat, the monkey, the rooster, the dog, and the pig. Each year has a different animal.

These animals are not the same. The dragon is a special animal in stories, but it is not a real animal. The other eleven are all true animals.

Some are big, like the ox and the horse. Some are small, like the rat and the rabbit.

People say each animal has a different meaning. The ox is a hard worker. The tiger is brave. The rabbit is quiet and gentle. The horse is energetic and fast. The monkey is smart, and the sheep... I mean the goat, is soft and kind.

Many families put a picture of the year's animal on their door at Chinese New Year. It is a fun sign for the new year. People also give red envelopes with the zodiac animal on them.

So, what is your zodiac animal? Are you an ox, a rabbit, or maybe a dragon? Next time you see the twelve zodiac animals, you will know their story!

Our Rooftop Garden

短文
📋 先看題目（聽完再作答）
  1. Where is the small garden?
  2. What does Ruby find near the gate?
  3. What do Ruby and her grandmother make for lunch?
語速：
分段聽：

On the roof of Ruby's apartment building, there is a small garden. Every Sunday, Ruby and her grandmother work there together. "There are many plants here today," Ruby says.

"There is a new tomato plant near the gate," her grandmother says. "Can you find it?" Ruby looks around. "Yes, I can see it! There are three small tomatoes on it," she says happily.

"There are also some carrots beside the wall," her grandmother says. "Please pull one out and wash it," she says. Ruby pulls a big carrot and washes it with water.

"There is a bee near the flowers. Don't worry, it is friendly," her grandmother says. Ruby watches the bee for a while. "There are so many flowers this week," she says.

At noon, they rest under a small tree on the roof. "There is a nice view of the whole city from here," Ruby says. "You can also see the school playground," her grandmother says.

"I'm hungry now," Ruby says. "There are some fresh tomatoes. Let us make a sandwich," her grandmother says with a smile. They cut the tomatoes and put them on bread.

"This is the best part of Sunday," Ruby says as she eats. Her grandmother agrees. There is always something new to find in their small rooftop garden every week.

Swimming Class with a New Coach

📋 先看題目（聽完再作答）
  1. What day is Kai's swimming class?
  2. Who is the new coach?
  3. What rule does Coach Lin tell the class?

分段聽：

It is Tuesday, and Kai's class has a swimming class at the school pool. "There are so many students here today," Kai says to his friend Ella.

"There is a new coach this week," Ella says. "His name is Coach Lin. He is friendly but a little strict." Kai nods and puts his bag near the wall.

"There are four groups today," Coach Lin says. "Please find your group and stand near the pool." Kai finds his group beside the big clock.

"There is a rule in this pool: don't run, and don't push," Coach Lin says. "Walk carefully to the water." Everyone listens and walks slowly to the pool.

In the water, Kai tries a new way to swim. "Can you swim to that line?" Coach Lin asks. "Yes, I can!" Kai says, and he swims fast to the other side.

"There is a small rest area beside the pool," Ella says. After class, they sit there and drink water. "I'm hungry now," Kai says. "There is a sandwich in my bag," Ella says with a smile.

"This class is hard but really fun," Kai says. "There is another swimming class next Tuesday," Coach Lin says. Kai and Ella are excited for the next class already.
