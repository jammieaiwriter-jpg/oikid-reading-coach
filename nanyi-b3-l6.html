原創課次文本，用本課單字和文法（未來式、感官動詞＋受詞＋原形/現在分詞）寫成（課本課文請看課本跟著念）。一次念一整段，念完做理解題——題目從這篇文字就能作答。
全文（點任一字查中文意思）

My uncle Alan is a singer in a modern band. Tonight he is going to visit our apartment before his big concert. I feel very happy, because someday I will be a musician too.

Alan wants to be successful with his music. He often uses new technology, like digital tools and virtual concerts on the Internet. He tells me that technology will shape the future of music.

After dinner, I watch Alan play his guitar. I also hear him sing a new song. He gives me a short guitar lesson, and I try his violin too. The music sounds really interesting.

The next morning, the news says a typhoon is coming tonight. Alan feels worried, because the concert is outside. We must be careful and check the weather again this afternoon.

That evening, we order pizza and hamburgers for dinner. We sit on the couch under a warm lamp and talk about my future. Alan says college will teach me many things.

That day, my school also has an earthquake drill. It is quick and simple, and nobody gets hurt. That night, I practice the song's lyrics and melody again and again.

Alan tells me a story from his own life. Years ago, he almost gave up his dream, but a band leader helped him again. Now Alan has his own small music company.

In the end, the typhoon does not come, and the concert can complete as planned. My grandma makes hot tea with honey for everyone backstage. I was born to love music, and I will follow my heart.

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：未來式（＝知洞 GP-35）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
未來式表示未來的計畫、預測或臨時決定：will + 原形動詞（臨時決定／預測）、be going to + 原形動詞（事先計畫）。問句：Will you...? / Are you going to...?；否定句：will not (won't) / am not (isn't/aren't) going to。常搭配時間副詞：tonight, tomorrow, next week, next year, soon, in the future。規則記好直接作答，每題答完把整句唸出來。

At first, the robot could only draw straight lines. My friend Nate worked on it every day after school. He never gave up, even when the robot made mistakes again and again.

One afternoon, something amazing happened. The robot drew a perfect circle, and then a small flower. Everyone in our class cheered loudly. Nate smiled and said, We did it together!

I learned an important lesson that day. If you keep trying, even a small idea can grow into something wonderful. I hope our robot will draw even better pictures next year.
