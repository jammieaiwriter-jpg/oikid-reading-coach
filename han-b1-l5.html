Every year, our family has a big calendar on the wall. It shows all twelve months, from January to December. My mom says, 'First, second, third, fourth—every date is important.' We can see all the important days at one time.
唸 2

唸 3
April is my birthday month. My birthday is on the third of April this year. I am a little nervous before the party, but I am also happy. My friends and family come around my house to celebrate.
唸 4
Summer comes next, in July and August. School is over, so we have free time. We only think about fun in July and August. When summer ends, September and October come, and school starts.
唸 5
November is Thanksgiving month. On the fourth Thursday, we eat a big dinner together. Everyone gets a turkey leg on their plate! We are thankful for our family, our home, and our warm meals.
唸 6
December is the last month, and Christmas is the best holiday. On the twenty-fifth of December, we open our presents. My little sister likes to hide before she gives us our gifts. What a happy year!

區塊 6 / 6　⑥ GEPT 延伸閱讀：A Year on Grandpa's Farm講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 185 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程：①整篇讀過一遍，遇到生字用上下文猜 → ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂 → ③生字補充 → ④逐段朗讀收尾。情境是農場四季，跟①區的節日行事曆情境不同。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Every year, Grandpa's farm changes with the seasons. In spring, the grass is green, and everything feels new. A big cow and her baby walk near the house. Grandpa says, 'Watch your step — there is mud everywhere after the rain!'

In summer, the sun is hot, and the animals stay near the trees for shade. The cow drinks cool water every morning. My little sister and I run around the farm and play games. We are happy on hot summer days.

Autumn comes next, and the weather is not so hot. The grass turns brown, and the animals eat more food before winter. Grandpa says the cow is very strong and never gets cold, even at night.

Winter is cold, and sometimes it snows on the farm. The cow stays inside a warm room. Grandpa gives her fresh grass and clean water every day. I love winter visits to the farm the most!

My family visits Grandpa's farm four times a year, once in every season. We always learn something new about the animals. The cow is gentle and never runs away from us.

生字補充（讀完文章了，來確認剛才用上下文猜的字）
單字
shade n.　陰涼處、樹蔭
最後朗讀（答完題才唸，一次一整段）
唸 1
Every year, Grandpa's farm changes with the seasons. In spring, the grass is green, and everything feels new. A big cow and her baby walk near the house. Grandpa says, 'Watch your step — there is mud everywhere after the rain!'
唸 2
In summer, the sun is hot, and the animals stay near the trees for shade. The cow drinks cool water every morning. My little sister and I run around the farm and play games. We are happy on hot summer days.
唸 3
Autumn comes next, and the weather is not so hot. The grass turns brown, and the animals eat more food before winter. Grandpa says the cow is very strong and never gets cold, even at night.
唸 4
Winter is cold, and sometimes it snows on the farm. The cow stays inside a warm room. Grandpa gives her fresh grass and clean water every day. I love winter visits to the farm the most!
唸 5
My family visits Grandpa's farm four times a year, once in every season. We always learn something new about the animals. The cow is gentle and never runs away from us.
