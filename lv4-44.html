Vocabulary Picture Talk

This man is scuba diving.

Fins and an air tank are the scuba diving gear he uses.

She is snorkeling.

A snorkel and goggles are the snorkeling gear she needs.

Go Fishing

James is going fishing.

He is very excited to catch some fish.

Can he catch fish with just his hands?

Of course not.

James needs a fishing rod, a line, a hook and bait to catch fish.

Fishing Gear

A fishing rod is a long, thin stick made of bamboo or steel with a reel and line.

It is used for catching fish.

Fishbait is food or an object used to attract fish to catch them.

Outdoor Activities

Water activities include fishing, sailing, scuba diving, surfing, swimming, snorkeling, and rowing.

Non-water activities include basketball, rollerblading, ice-skating, soccer, tennis, badminton, and biking.

Compare and Contrast

Indoor activities happen inside, and outdoor activities happen outside.

Some activities can happen in a gym, on a field, or in a swimming pool.
