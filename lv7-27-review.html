Grammar Check
Grammar 1
Rewrite in passive voice: I saw a dog on the street.
先把 a dog 放到前面，再用 was seen。
Grammar 2
Rewrite in passive voice: She listens to the radio every day.
注意 listen 要保留 to。
Grammar 3
Rewrite in passive voice: She listened to the music.
過去式 passive 用 was listened to。
Grammar 4
Rewrite in passive voice: I saw a snake eat a mouse.
Sense verb type 2 用 get eaten。
