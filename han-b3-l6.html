原創課次文本，用本課單字和文法（未來式、特殊動詞用法）寫成（課本課文請看課本跟著念）。一次念一整段，念完做理解題——題目從這篇文字就能作答。
全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Tomorrow is my cousin's wedding, and I need something nice to wear. My old clothes are too small now. This afternoon, Mom and I will go to the mall to find a new outfit.

At the mall, we walk past a shop with cheap T-shirts and a shop with expensive dresses. Mom says we should not spend too much money, so we look at the price tags first.

I try on a blue shirt and black pants. The shirt fits well, but the pants are too long. A worker says she will make them shorter for a small fee.

Next, I look at shoes and socks. Most shoes here cost between one and two thousand dollars. I find a pair of black shoes that are not too expensive, and I really like them.

Mom picks a nice dress for herself. She also buys a small ring and some new glasses. She says fashion is fun, but she still wants to save some money for later.

My little brother wants a funny hat and a pair of gloves, even though the weather will still be warm tomorrow. Dad tells him the truth: he will not need gloves in this hot weather.

We also choose a tie and a belt for my dad. The cap by the door looks a bit ugly, so nobody buys it. We laugh and walk to the checkout counter.

At the checkout, the total is high, but not too high. Mom pays with cash, and the worker gives her some change. Mom says this shop is good for the environment because it reuses old bags.

Tomorrow morning, we will wear our new clothes to the wedding. I will put on my new shirt, pants, and shoes. I think anything can happen at a wedding, but I am ready for a great day!

全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Before we go shopping, Mom says almost anything at this shop is nice, but I still want a new belt and a cool cap, so I bring some cash just in case.

I have some change in my pocket, and the shop has a cheap coat near the checkout counter, so I decide to buy it before we leave.

This dress does not cost too much, and it is not expensive at all; buying good clothes here is also good for the environment because the shop reuses old bags.

My cousin loves fashion and always wears fast, funny clothes; she puts on her glasses and laughs at herself in the big mirror.

Dad picks a warm glove and a soft hat for the cold days, but the shelf is too high for me, so he helps me choose a new pair of jeans too.

At the mall, most shops have both high and low prices, so I take my time to find a good pair of shoes.

I pay for a new pair of pants with my card, and I check the price twice before I get a nice ring for my mom.

We save time by taking the elevator to the second floor, where I try on a blue shirt and a white shoe.

In summer, I like to wear shorts and a light skirt, and I always need a new pair of socks; I try not to spend too much on socks though.

The store is still open at night, and there is a warm sweater and a soft T-shirt for less than one thousand dollars.

Dad picks a striped tie for tomorrow's wedding, and the clerk tells us the total price; to tell the truth, it is a fair price.

The old shoes at the corner look a little ugly, so nobody wants to wear them, but Mom says she will try something new.

區塊 3 / 8　③ 文法 A1：will 未來式（＝知洞 GP-35）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
will + 動詞原形，表示「將要」發生的事，常用來說臨時決定或預測：will not（won't）+ 動詞原形是否定句，Will + 主詞 + 動詞原形...? 是疑問句。動詞一律用原形，不能用 will goes。規則記好直接作答，每題答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 4 / 8　④ 文法 A2：be going to 未來式（＝知洞 GP-35）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）

Last Saturday, my aunt opened a small clothes shop near our house. She asked our whole family to help her on the first day.

My job was easy. I stood by the door and gave a paper bag to every customer who bought something. My cousin helped people find the right size.

In the morning, many people came in. Some looked at the price tags and left, but many others found something they liked. One woman bought three shirts at the same time.

At noon, an old man came in and asked for a warm coat. My aunt helped him choose one, and he paid with cash. He said the coat felt very soft and warm.

In the afternoon, a boy about my age came with his mom. He wanted a cap, but it did not fit him well. My aunt found a smaller one, and he smiled happily.

By evening, my aunt looked tired but happy. She said the shop did better than she expected on the first day. My family and I were happy to help her.

Before we went home, my aunt gave us each a small gift from the shop. Mine was a pair of soft socks. I will wear them tomorrow and remember this special day.
