# 📘 【知識內容區】

### Ⅰ. 核心單字 (Vocabulary)
請掌握以下 4 個與旅行及露營相關的關鍵詞彙：

1.  **campsite** (n.)：露營地。例句：We slept at a campsite at night.
2.  **highway** (n.)：公路。例句：They drove on the highway from place to place.
3.  **scenery** (n.)：風景。例句：They saw beautiful scenery along the way.
4.  **hammock** (n.)：吊床。例句：Lying in a hammock was really relaxing.

### Ⅱ. 口說短文 (Reading Coach)
請練習朗讀以下短文，注意語調的連貫性：

> The Johnson family took a trip to Taiwan. They drove on the highway from place to place and saw beautiful scenery along the way. At night, they stayed at a campsite. Mr. Johnson enjoyed relaxing in his hammock, and they used a tarp to keep the rain off their baggage. They had a wonderful time visiting famous landmarks.
