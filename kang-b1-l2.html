Our class has a flea market today. My classmates bring toys and books from home. Look at this table! It has many toy animals on it.

This toy bear is soft and brown. That toy dragon is red and long. Are these toy zebras yours? No, they are not mine.

A boy asks, "What is this?" His friend says, "It is a toy fox. It is only two dollars." They both laugh and shake hands.

On the next table, there are toy horses and toy oxen. A girl says, "These are my favorite toys, but I don't need them now."

"What are those?" a small kid asks, pointing far away. "Those are toy sheep," the seller says. "They are soft and very cute."

My little brother buys a toy elephant and a toy lion. He is a real people person, so he also talks with many people at the market.

This toy monkey is energetic and smart. That toy rabbit looks calm and quiet. A toy mouse and a toy rat sit together in a small box.

A toy rooster and a toy snake are only one meter from the door. Their price sign says, "Buy two, get one free!"

At the end of the day, my brother says, "This was a true success! I got many different animal toys today."

This toy monkey is energetic and smart. That toy rabbit looks calm and quiet. The monkey jumps up and down, but the rabbit just sits still.

Look at this little mouse and that little rat! A real mouse has a short lifespan, but this toy mouse never gets old. Is that true?

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：指示代名詞 this / that / these / those（＝知洞 GP-04）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
指近處、單數用 this；指遠處、單數用 that；指近處、複數用 these；指遠處、複數用 those。回答 What is this/that? 用 It is...；回答 What are these/those? 用 They are...。直接作答，答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 4 / 6　④ 文法 B：名詞複數規則（＝知洞 GP-02）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
規則變化加 s/es；不規則變化如 ox→oxen、mouse→mice；單複數同形如 sheep；不可數名詞（如 money）不能直接加 s。直接作答，答完把整句唸出來。

【挑戰閱讀】約 200 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。情境跟課文的跳蚤市集不同，是另一個生活情境（奶奶的麵包店）。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Every Saturday morning, I help my grandmother at her small bakery. The shop opens at seven, and the smell of fresh bread fills the air. I wear a blue apron and put warm rolls in the window.

My grandmother makes the same bread every week, but she also tries new recipes. Last week, she made a round loaf with cheese inside. It was a big hit with our neighbors.

A young man comes in and asks for two loaves of bread. He says he is baking a cake for his sister's birthday, so he also buys some sugar and butter from our small shelf.

An old woman visits us every Saturday. She always orders the same order: one loaf of bread and a cup of tea. She sits by the window and reads her newspaper slowly.

At noon, the shop gets busy. Many people line up outside the door. My grandmother works fast, but she still smiles at every customer. I learn that a good baker needs patience and a kind heart.

Before we close, we count the money and clean the counter. My grandmother says the best part of her day is seeing happy faces leave with warm bread. I feel proud to help her.
