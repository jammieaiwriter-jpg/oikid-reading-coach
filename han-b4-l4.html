全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Last night, at eleven o'clock, the ground suddenly began to shake. My whole body started to sway, and I heard a strange, sharp noise coming from the kitchen.

My little brother's face turned pale with fear. Mom said we should get under the table, and Dad told us we must stay calm until the shaking stopped.

The lights suddenly went out. Dad quickly found a flashlight, and Mom lit a candle on the table. The dark room felt as quiet as empty space.

When the shaking finally stopped, we turned on the radio to hear the latest news. The reporter said this earthquake felt several times stronger than last month's typhoon winds.

Through the window, I saw a giant crack across our garden wall. The pond outside looked calm, but I could still sense fear inside me, even though the noise had stopped.

The next morning, our neighbor Mr. Lin was still missing. If his old house had fallen, he might be trapped under the rubble. We knew we must go and help him.

Dad said we should prepare some water and a flashlight before we walked over. Although the street was covered with rubble, we bravely found a narrow space between two broken walls.

Through that narrow space, we heard a weak voice calling for help. It was Mr. Lin! Rescue workers reached him and pulled him out slowly. He was a lucky survivor, and his face looked calm again.

That evening, a pretty rainbow appeared above the garden, and the frogs by the pond began to sing again. I told myself I would always prepare for the next earthquake, and I felt proud of what we did.

Every family must prepare for an earthquake, and everyone should know what to do if the ground starts to shake.

You should always prepare a small bag for yourself, and I always prepare one for myself too.

Last year, a strong typhoon hit our town, and the wind felt many times more powerful than a normal storm.

In our garden, there is a small pond with a pretty rose bush beside it, and at night we can hear a frog singing near the water.

The garden is not very big in size, but at night the stars shine brightly above it, and it feels as peaceful as open space.

My uncle used to train rescue dogs, and he says a brave dog can find people bravely, even in the most dangerous places.

After the storm, a colorful rainbow often appears in the sky, and it always makes people feel hopeful again.

區塊 3 / 8　③ 文法 A1：must 的用法（＝知洞 GP-36）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
must + 動詞原形：表示「必須／一定」，可以是強烈的規定或義務，也可以是很有把握的推測。否定 must not（mustn't）表示「禁止」（跟 don't have to「不必」不同）。must 不隨主詞變化，沒有 musts、musting。每題答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 4 / 8　④ 文法 A2：should 的用法（＝知洞 GP-36）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
should + 動詞原形：表示「應該」，是建議或提醒，語氣比 must 弱一些（must 是規定／必須，should 是建議）。否定 should not（shouldn't）。動詞一律用原形，不能用 should goes。每題答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 6 / 8　⑥ 文法 B2：介系詞 despite 的用法（＝知洞 GP-57）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）

Every Friday evening, my grandpa sells fruit at the night market near our home. Last week, I went to help him for the first time.

My job was easy. I stood next to the fruit stand and gave a small paper bag to every person who bought something.

It began to rain after eight o'clock. Despite the rain, many people still came to buy fruit, and grandpa smiled at each one of them.

A young man asked for the sweetest apples we had. Grandpa picked five red apples and put them in a bag for him.

An old woman came near the end of the night. She said the price was a little high, so grandpa gave her a small discount.

Although we were both tired, grandpa and I kept working until almost every apple was sold. We talked and laughed between customers.

At the end of the night, grandpa gave me a warm drink and said I was a good helper. I felt proud of myself.

Before we packed up the stand, grandpa gave me two apples to take home. I will remember that rainy night for a long time.
