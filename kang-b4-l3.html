全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Last week, a new restaurant opened on Grandview Street. A big panda sign hung on the wall next to the sidewalk, so people noticed it right away. My friend Leo invited me to try the new place soon.

When we walked in, the smell of fresh bread and hot coffee filled the room. The owner was very friendly, and she said, "Besides pizza, we also make hamburgers and sandwiches here."

Leo ordered a ham sandwich with a bun and cheese, but I could not decide. The menu had bean soup, vegetable soup, steak, and salad, so I asked the owner what she liked best.

"Try the fried chicken with French fries," she said. "It tastes great, and I promise it does not use too much oil." I took her advice and ordered fried chicken, French fries, and coffee.

The fried chicken tasted delicious, and the French fries were crispy, not oily at all. Leo took a big bite of his ham sandwich and said it tasted better than any sandwich he had before.

After the meal, the sky outside became dark, and it started to matter to us how late it was. The friendly owner let us stay a little longer and get some toast with sugar.

Leo joked that eating too much meat and pork might change his weight, but he probably would not stop eating hamburgers anytime soon. I laughed and took one more bite of toast.

Before we left, we noticed the owner feeding a real panda toy on the counter near the wall. We agreed the new diner would soon become our favorite place on the whole street.

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：連綴動詞（＝知洞 GP-41）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
連綴動詞（linking verbs）like become、get、taste、smell、sound、look、feel 後面接「形容詞」，不接副詞：This soup tastes great.／The sky gets dark.／My legs feel tired. 主詞是第三人稱單數或不可數名詞時動詞要加 -s（tastes/smells/sounds/looks/feels/gets/becomes）。答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 4 / 6　④ 文法 B：感官動詞與使役動詞基礎用法（＝知洞 GP-43．基礎面向）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
本課先學基礎面向：感官動詞（see/hear/smell/notice/watch）+受詞+原形動詞或V-ing（表示看到/聽到/聞到整個過程用原形，看到/聽到/聞到進行中的一部分用V-ing）；使役動詞 make/have/let +受詞+原形動詞（表示使、讓某人做某事，主詞第三人稱單數要在 make/have/let 上加 -s，受詞後的動詞仍用原形）。進階面向（get/have+受詞+過去分詞的使役被動用法，例如 get one's hair cut）留到 L5 教，這裡先打好基礎。答完把整句唸出來。

先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Last month, our school started a new cooking class every Friday afternoon. My friend Owen and I joined right away because we both love food.

On the first day, the teacher put fresh bread, cheese, and ham on the table. She said, "Besides sandwiches, today we will also learn to make a simple salad."

Owen wanted to cook first, so he made a ham sandwich with cheese and a bun. He let the bread toast a little, and the whole room began to smell wonderful.

I decided to try something different. I mixed lettuce, tomatoes, and a little oil to make a fresh salad. The teacher watched me work and said it looked colorful.

After twenty minutes, everyone tasted each other's food. Owen's sandwich tasted amazing, and my salad tasted fresh and light. We both agreed our meal was a success.

The teacher then showed us a photo of a famous cook. She explained that this cook became well known for simple, healthy meals, not heavy food with too much oil.

Owen said he probably eats too much meat and not enough vegetables. He promised to eat more salad and less fried food to keep a healthy weight.

Before class ended, the teacher let us invite one more classmate to join next week. Owen and I agreed to invite our friend Mia because she loves trying new food.

We left the classroom hungry but happy. Cooking together helped us notice how much fun food can be, and we cannot wait for the next Friday class.
