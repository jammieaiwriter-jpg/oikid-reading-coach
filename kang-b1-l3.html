區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：祈使句、感嘆句（＝知洞 GP-12）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
祈使句：直接用原形動詞開頭，命令或請求，例如 Look!／Don't touch it.／Let's go!；也可加 please 讓語氣更客氣。感嘆句：What + a/an + 形容詞 + 名詞（+主詞動詞）!，或 How + 形容詞/副詞（+主詞動詞）!。規則記好直接作答，每題答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 4 / 6　④ 文法 B：what／who／where 問句與答句（＝知洞 GP-06）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）

區塊 6 / 6　⑥ GEPT 延伸閱讀：A Photo for the School Contest講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 250 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內，情境跟課文（露營野炊）不同，換成校內攝影比賽。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Every year, Mia's school has a photo contest for the students. This year, the theme is 'Our City in the Morning.' Mia has a good idea. She wants to take pictures of the old morning market near her house.

On Saturday, Mia gets up early with her father. The city is quiet at six o'clock. Only a few people are near the market. Mia takes out her phone and looks around for a good picture.

She sees a woman selling fruit. She sees a man with a cart of vegetables. 'What a nice morning!' Mia says. She takes many pictures of the market and the quiet street.

At home, Mia looks at all her pictures. She must choose only one for the contest. Some pictures are too dark. Some are not clear. 'Which one is the best?' she asks her father.

Her father has an idea. 'Try this picture tool,' he says. 'It can make the picture bright.' Mia does not have an expensive camera, but the free tool on her phone works well.

She picks her favorite picture: the woman selling fruit under the morning sun. Mia gives the picture a name: 'A New Day.' Then she uploads it to the school's contest website.

A week later, Mia gets a message. Her teacher asks her a question: 'Can you tell the class about your picture?' Mia is very happy. Her picture wins first place! Her small city, in her small picture, is now known around the world.

最後朗讀（答完題才唸，一次一整段）
唸 1
Every year, Mia's school has a photo contest for the students. This year, the theme is 'Our City in the Morning.' Mia has a good idea. She wants to take pictures of the old morning market near her house.
唸 2
On Saturday, Mia gets up early with her father. The city is quiet at six o'clock. Only a few people are near the market. Mia takes out her phone and looks around for a good picture.
唸 3
She sees a woman selling fruit. She sees a man with a cart of vegetables. 'What a nice morning!' Mia says. She takes many pictures of the market and the quiet street.
唸 4
At home, Mia looks at all her pictures. She must choose only one for the contest. Some pictures are too dark. Some are not clear. 'Which one is the best?' she asks her father.
唸 5
Her father has an idea. 'Try this picture tool,' he says. 'It can make the picture bright.' Mia does not have an expensive camera, but the free tool on her phone works well.
唸 6
She picks her favorite picture: the woman selling fruit under the morning sun. Mia gives the picture a name: 'A New Day.' Then she uploads it to the school's contest website.
唸 7
A week later, Mia gets a message. Her teacher asks her a question: 'Can you tell the class about your picture?' Mia is very happy. Her picture wins first place! Her small city, in her small picture, is now known around the world.
