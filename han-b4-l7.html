區塊 1 / 6　① 課文朗讀：Remembering Loved Ones Around the World（原創）講義 原創課次對應文本（世界各地紀念亡者的節日）
原創課次文本，用本課單字寫成（課本課文請看課本跟著念）。一次念一整段，念完做理解題——題目從這篇文字就能作答。主題是墨西哥亡靈節與印尼托拉查人的家族傳統，語氣正面、溫馨，不是恐怖故事。
全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Every November, many families in Mexico celebrate a special holiday called the Day of the Dead. This celebration is not sad. Families remember their loved ones who have passed away, and they feel happy and thankful.

During the celebration, families visit the graves of their loved ones. They clean each grave and bring fresh flowers, candles, and the person's favorite food. They want the grave to look bright and beautiful.

Many people also paint their faces to look like a skull. This is not scary to them. A skull face reminds everyone that life and death are both a natural part of the family story.

Every family member joins the celebration together, from grandparents to small children. They tell stories about the person they miss, and they laugh about happy memories. The whole family stays together all night.

Far away in Indonesia, the Torajan people also hold a special celebration for their loved ones. Every few years, they open the family grave and clean the body of a family member who has died.

The Torajan family gives the body fresh clothes and takes new photos together. To them, this is not frightening at all. It is a way to show love and to continue a strong family tradition.

Both celebrations show something important: families in Mexico and Indonesia continue to love their family members even after death. They do not want to forget the people who were once part of their lives.

These celebrations teach us that death does not have to be a scary topic. Around the world, different cultures have their own special ways to remember loved ones and to celebrate a life well lived.

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：both...and／neither...nor（＝知洞 GP-58 面向一）講義 原創題（他版已教、翰林尚未教的考點：對等相關連接詞）

區塊 6 / 6　⑥ GEPT 延伸閱讀：A New Friend at the Shelter講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 210 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內，情境與課文（世界紀念亡者的節日）無關，是獨立情境（動物收容所志工的故事）。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Leo visits the animal shelter every Saturday morning. He likes to help the workers clean the cages and feed the dogs and cats. The shelter is always busy, and the animals are happy to see him.

One Saturday, Leo finds a new puppy in a small cage. The puppy is thin and quiet, and it does not want to play with the other dogs. The workers say no one wants to adopt it yet.

Leo decides to spend extra time with the puppy every week. He sits next to the cage and talks to it in a gentle voice. Slowly, the puppy starts to feel more comfortable with him.

After a few weeks, the puppy becomes much more friendly. It runs to the front of the cage and looks happy when it hears Leo's voice. The workers are surprised by the big change.

One afternoon, a family visits the shelter looking for a gentle dog. Leo tells them about the puppy and how much it likes attention now. The family falls in love with it right away.

The family decides to adopt the puppy that same day. Leo feels proud and a little sad to see it go, but he knows the puppy finally has a warm home. He still visits it every month.
