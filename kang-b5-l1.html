全文（點任一字查中文意思）

This month, Mr. Chen's class has started a big project about holidays around the world. Every student has chosen one country and must explain how people there celebrate a special day.

Ivy picked the United States. She has learned that Americans have celebrated Thanksgiving since the seventeenth century. It is a national holiday, and a big meal is still common that day.

Tom chose an island country instead. He said people there speak a different language, and their king still visits soldiers and schools every year on the national holiday.

For her report, Mia baked cookies and made hot chocolate as a snack for the whole class. She has never baked cookies before, but her first try was excellent.

Jack talked about Halloween. In many areas, children carry a small pumpkin and knock on doors for candy. Some families even fry potato chips as a special snack that night.

Ben's topic was Easter. He said nobody in his family has ever painted eggs before, so this year they tried it for the first time with paper and paint.

During the presentation, Lily's hairdryer story made everyone laugh. She explained that in winter, some lakes freeze so hard that people can walk on the thick ice, inch by inch.

Mr. Chen said the main goal of the project is to compare different traditions. He has not finished checking every report yet, but he already thinks this class did a great job.

At the end of class, Mia stood up and gave a big wave to everyone. "Thank you for listening," she said. "I hope our project was successful."

Mr. Chen agreed. He said this has been one of the most interesting classes he has taught in years, and he looks forward to reading every report soon.

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：現在完成式 have/has + p.p.（＝知洞 GP-49・基礎面向）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
GP-49（現在完成式）版內分工：本課（L1）先教基礎面向——構造為 have/has + 過去分詞，主詞為 I/you/we/they/複數用 have，he/she/it/單數用 has；搭配 already（已經，肯定句）、yet（還沒，否定句/疑問句句尾）、never/ever（從未／曾經，表經驗）。since/for 表持續時間的進階面向留到文法 B。答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 4 / 6　④ 文法 B：現在完成式 since／for（＝知洞 GP-49・進階面向）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
since 後面接「一個時間點」（如 since 1621、since Monday、since I was born，也可接一個完整子句）；for 後面接「一段時間長度」（如 for three days、for ten years）。兩者都常與現在完成式連用，表示動作或狀態從過去持續到現在。答完把整句唸出來。

【挑戰閱讀】約 220 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Every spring, Green Hill Elementary School holds a small book fair in the gym. Students can look at new books and trade old ones they no longer read.

This year, a new student named Owen helped the librarian set up the tables. He carried boxes of books from the front office to the gym before school started.

At first, Owen did not know many students at his new school. He felt a little shy when he stood behind the table alone.

During lunch, a girl named Priya stopped by the table. She asked Owen which books he liked best, and they talked about stories for almost ten minutes.

By the end of the week, Owen had made three new friends who also loved reading. They planned to meet at the library every Friday afternoon.

On the last day of the book fair, the school gave a small prize to the class that traded the most books. Owen's class won because so many students joined in.

The librarian thanked Owen for his help and asked him to be a helper again next year. Owen felt proud and happy about his new school.

That night, Owen told his parents that moving to a new school was hard at first, but the book fair helped him find kind new friends quickly.
