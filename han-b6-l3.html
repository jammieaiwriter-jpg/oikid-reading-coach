全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Every year, our school holds a science fair in March. This year, my group's mission was to study the flu pandemic of 1918 and compare it with modern technology.

Before we began, our teacher told us to pick a clear question. We picked this one: how does technology help scientists stop a pandemic faster today?

While we did our research, we found many old newspapers about the 1918 flu pandemic. A local scientist also agreed to answer our questions by email.

Although the research was hard, we did not give up. We looked into old records and wrote down every important point we found.

As soon as we finished the reading part, we started to build our poster. My job was to draw a chart that showed the speed of the disease spreading in 1918.

When the scientist wrote back, she explained that new technology today can find a new disease much faster than in 1918. That was a key point for our project.

Despite a few problems with our first chart, we did not throw it away. We just fixed the numbers and tried again until it looked clear and correct.

On the day of the science fair, the judges walked around and looked at every poster. They gave our group a small present: a card that said, "Best Mission, Best Team!"

區塊 4 / 7　④ 文法 B：連接詞 if／(al)though 與介系詞 despite（＝知洞 GP-57）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點，進階應用與易錯點）

區塊 5 / 7　⑤ 文法 C：從屬連接詞 when/while/before/as soon as/until（＝知洞 GP-33）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點，進階應用與易錯點）
when（在……時，指某一刻）、while（當……的同時，常搭配進行式）、before（在……之前）、as soon as（一……就）、until（直到……為止）。容易搞混的是 when 跟 as soon as：as soon as 特別強調「馬上、緊接著」發生，when 只是單純的時間點。每題答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 7 / 7　⑦ GEPT 延伸閱讀：My First Mission at Winter Camp講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍，文章庫可換一篇）
【挑戰閱讀】約 270 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。可點「換一篇」輪替文章庫其他 3 篇（夏令營營火知識介紹／公園寫生比賽公告／演講比賽後台書信）。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Last January, I joined a three-day winter camp in the mountains. When I got off the bus, a leader gave every student a small card and said our first mission was to check in at the front desk.

I picked up my card and walked to the desk. A worker there had a new piece of technology, a small machine. It could read the card in one second and print my team name on it.

While I was waiting in line, I watched other students talk and laugh with their new friends. I was a little nervous, but I was also excited about the days ahead.

My team leader explained the point system. Each student could win points by finishing a mission, helping a friend, or reaching an activity early. The team with the most points would get a special present on the last night.

Before dinner, our team had to pick a team name and a team song. Although some of us were shy, we worked together and picked a name every team member wanted: the Snow Foxes.

That night, a scientist from a university near the camp gave a short talk about the science of ice and speed on the hills. Many students listened and asked questions.

As soon as the talk ended, our leader said we must hurry to our rooms. We had to be ready before nine o'clock, or our team would lose points.

I lay in bed and thought about the busy day. Checking in was only the first mission, but it already seemed like the start of a great winter camp.
