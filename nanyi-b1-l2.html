區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：what/who/where 引導的問句與答句（＝知洞 GP-06）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）

區塊 6 / 6　⑦ GEPT 延伸閱讀：A Night at the Market講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 200 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——遇到沒學過的字用上下文猜，不要停 → ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中） → ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。情境跟①課文不同（夜市之夜，非社團博覽會）。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Every Friday night, my family walks to the night market near our home. The street is full of colorful lights and lanterns. Many vendors sell food, toys, and clothes. My grandmother says the market smells wonderful.

First, we visit a food stand and buy hot noodles. My father likes the smell of the hot food, so he buys some for all of us. My little sister wants a candy apple, and my mother buys her one with a coin.

Next, we walk to a game corner. My brother tries to win a prize by hitting a ball into a small cup. He tries again and again, but he does not win. He is a little sad, but he smiles again when he sees a fish game.

At a small tofu shop, an old woman gives us a free taste. It is warm and delicious! We say thank you and buy a big box to share.

The market is busy and loud, but it is also fun. Music plays near the corner, and children dance and laugh. My grandmother buys a pretty fan for herself.

Before we go home, we stop at a quiet park near the market. We sit and eat our snacks together. The night is cold, but our hearts are warm. We are tired, but we had a wonderful night at the market.
