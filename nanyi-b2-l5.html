全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Every month, our school newsletter writes about a student athlete. This month, the reporter talks to Leo, a Grade 7 student. Leo swims for the school team, and he also joins the tug of war team.

The reporter asks Leo the first question: How often do you practice? Leo says he always trains early in the morning before class. He never skips a practice, even when he feels tired.

Sometimes Leo feels very sick after a hard practice, but he still goes to school. He says a good athlete cares about the team, not just about winning a trophy.

The reporter asks about tug of war. Leo says the team practices twice a week during lunch. Each player has a different job, and everyone works together to win.

Leo usually finishes his homework right after dinner. He says his life is busy, but he never forgets his schoolwork. His parents often ask about his grades, and he always answers with a smile.

Once a year, our school has a big sports day. Leo's swim team and tug of war team both join. Every time, Leo's swim team wins a nice trophy for the school.

The reporter has one more question: What is your dream? Leo says he wants to join a sports team in senior high school. He hopes to meet new friends there, too.

At the end of the interview, Leo gives the reporter some advice. He says, Put in real effort every day, and do not give up. Many students in our school now want to try a new sport.

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：頻率副詞的意義與次數（＝知洞 GP-17）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
頻率副詞由高到低：always（總是）> usually（通常）> often（經常）> sometimes（有時候）> seldom（很少）> never（從不）。表示次數也可用 once a week（一週一次）、twice a month（一個月兩次）、three times a week（一週三次）。先分辨意義，再作答，每題答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 4 / 6　④ 文法 B：頻率副詞的位置規則（＝知洞 GP-17）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
頻率副詞的位置：放在一般動詞（practice, eat, play…）前面；放在 be動詞／can 後面。例如：He often practices. He is often tired. He can often finish early。問「多常」用 How often…?，回答時用次數（once/twice/three times a week）。規則記好直接作答，答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 6 / 6　⑥ GEPT 延伸閱讀：The Reading Challenge Corner講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 200 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內，主題與課文《The School Newsletter》不同（圖書館閱讀挑戰角，不是校刊訪問運動員），也不撞 4 篇文章庫的場景。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Every Friday afternoon, a Grade 7 student named Mia visits the town library's Reading Challenge Corner. Students there can choose a new book to read that week.

Mia often visits the library after school. She usually reads a book in three or four days, and then she comes back for another one.

A library worker keeps a chart on the wall. Each time a student finishes a book, they get a small star next to their name. Mia never forgets to check her star.

Some students seldom finish a whole book, but they still get points for trying. The worker says trying is more important than finishing fast.

Once a month, the library gives a small prize to students with the most stars. Mia usually wins a new pencil, not a big prize, but she feels proud.

This month, Mia has a question for the worker: How often do new books arrive? The worker answers that new books usually arrive twice a month, on Mondays.

Mia always tells her classmates about good books. She says reading takes some effort, but it is also very fun. Many of her classmates now visit the library, too.

The worker notices more students in the Reading Challenge Corner these days. She says the corner is usually quiet, but now it feels busy and happy.

唸 1
Every Friday afternoon, a Grade 7 student named Mia visits the town library's Reading Challenge Corner. Students there can choose a new book to read that week.
唸 2
Mia often visits the library after school. She usually reads a book in three or four days, and then she comes back for another one.
唸 3
A library worker keeps a chart on the wall. Each time a student finishes a book, they get a small star next to their name. Mia never forgets to check her star.
唸 4
Some students seldom finish a whole book, but they still get points for trying. The worker says trying is more important than finishing fast.
唸 5
Once a month, the library gives a small prize to students with the most stars. Mia usually wins a new pencil, not a big prize, but she feels proud.
唸 6
This month, Mia has a question for the worker: How often do new books arrive? The worker answers that new books usually arrive twice a month, on Mondays.
唸 7
Mia always tells her classmates about good books. She says reading takes some effort, but it is also very fun. Many of her classmates now visit the library, too.
唸 8
The worker notices more students in the Reading Challenge Corner these days. She says the corner is usually quiet, but now it feels busy and happy.
