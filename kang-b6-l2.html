全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Last month, my family moved to a new apartment building. I did not know anyone there, and every neighbor felt like a stranger. I often felt shy and a little lonely after school.

One rainy afternoon, I walked home through mud and heavy traffic. I had no umbrella, and my shoes and bag were soon wet and dirty.

A man from next door, Mr. Lin, saw me and lent me his big umbrella. He was very polite and said, "Keep it as long as you need."

The next day, I returned the umbrella and borrowed a towel to dry my wet hair before I said thank you and walked back home.

Mr. Lin told me about his hobby: a neighborhood scavenger hunt club. Members meet every Saturday and hunt for small prizes around the park.

I joined the club that weekend. At first, I felt shy, but every member was friendly, so my nervous feeling did not last long.

During the game, we take turns to roll a die and decide which team searches first. It was finally my turn, and I rolled a six.

My team ran through the park and looked under benches and behind trees. Suddenly, someone shouted, "Oh my God, we found the prize box!"

That evening, my grandmother, a relative who lives nearby, visited and noticed my joy. I told her I was neither shy nor lonely anymore.

Now, I keep my room tidy every Friday night, because our scavenger hunt club meets at my apartment once a month. This hobby is now widely loved by our whole neighborhood.

區塊 3 / 7　③ 文法 A1：非限定關係子句（逗號＋補充資訊）（＝知洞 GP-56・複習冊進階面向一）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
GP-56 版內分工：kang-b5-l5 教主格關代（who/which/that 當主詞）、b5-l6 教受格關代（可省略）與所有格 whose／介系詞＋關代——這些都是「限定」關係子句（拿掉子句會改變句子指的是誰／哪個）。本課（複習冊）聚焦會考常考、b5 兩課沒教的面向：非限定關係子句——用逗號把子句隔開，子句只是「補充說明」，拿掉它主句意思仍完整、指稱對象不變；非限定子句只能用 who／which／whose，絕對不能用 that。規則記好直接作答，每題答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 4 / 7　④ 文法 A2：關係代名詞合併長句（會考句子合併應用）（＝知洞 GP-56・複習冊進階面向二）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
GP-56 複習冊進階面向二：把兩個各自獨立的短句合併成一句更長的關係子句句子——這是會考閱讀和寫作常考的「句子合併」應用題型。方法：找出兩句共同的名詞，把第二句改成 who／which／that（或 whose）子句，接在第一句共同名詞後面。跟 gramA1 的非限定子句是不同面向：這裡練的是「怎麼把兩句變一句」，不是逗號用法。答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 5 / 7　⑤ 文法 B：附和句「也」的說法（＝知洞 GP-55）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
GP-55：用「也」附和別人說的話。附和肯定句：So + 助動詞/be動詞 + 主詞（倒裝，如 So am I. / So does she.），或句尾直接加 ", too."；附和否定句：Neither + 助動詞/be動詞 + 主詞（倒裝，如 Neither am I. / Neither did she.），或句尾直接加 ", either."。規則記好直接作答，答完把整句唸出來。

先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Behind the school gym, there is a quiet corner with a few benches and a small garden. Every afternoon, a group of stray cats gathers there to rest in a cool, quiet spot.

Last spring, a shy new student called Wu Ting joined the animal care club. She was like a stranger at first, but animals always made her happy, so she wanted to help the cats near the gym.

The club members feed the cats every morning before class. They bring bowls of food and clean water, and they keep the feeding area tidy so insects will not come near.

One of the cats, a small gray kitten, was too shy to come close to anyone. The other members said this kitten needed a gentle, patient friend.

Every day, Wu Ting waited near the same spot and stayed silent. She did not try to touch the kitten. She only talked in a low, gentle voice, even when she was tired.

After two weeks, the kitten walked nearer and nearer. One morning, it finally came right up to Wu Ting and let her touch its gray coat for the first time.

The whole club was full of joy that day. They put a story about the kitten on the notice board of the club so other students could read about a stray cat that learned to trust again.

Now, this small, curious kitten follows Wu Ting around the campus after school. Wu Ting says she learned that staying patient, not fast, wins the trust of a stray animal.
