區塊 3 / 7　③ 文法 A：be 動詞（＝知洞 GP-01）講義 原創題（對應課本 p.21 文法點）

區塊 7 / 7　⑦ GEPT 延伸閱讀：A Day at the Zoo講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 210 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。 短文裡有幾個還沒教的句型（如 loves、are eating）——看懂大意就好，不列入本課文法要求。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

My name is Ben. I am a student. Today is Sunday, and my family is at the zoo. My little sister is very happy. She loves animals.

Look! What are those? my sister asks. They are zebras, I say. They are black and white. The zebras are eating grass. They are not fat, but they are strong.

Next, we see a big animal in the water. It is brown and very fat. Is it a bear? No, it isn't. It is a hippo. Hippos love water. They stay in the water because it is hot today.

Then we walk to a small house. There are many birds in it. Some are red, and some are blue. A green bird says hello to us! My sister laughs and says hello, too. It is a smart bird.

Before we go home, we see the monkeys. A baby monkey is on its mother's back. It has a banana in its hand. Monkeys are funny animals.

What is your favorite animal? I ask. I love the hippo! she says. It is big and cute. My favorite is the smart bird. It says hello, just like a friend! We are tired but very happy. What a fun day!
