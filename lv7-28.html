Reading Passage Shadowing
Festival Basics

People have organized festivals for thousands of years.

Festivals are events celebrated by a particular group, or the entire country, and it is centered on some particular tradition.

Most, but not all festivals are held to provide entertainment to the participants, so they are inclined to be complemented with music or dance.

The majority of festivals are religious or based on folklore.

Pingxi Lantern Festival

Pingxi Lantern Festival started in 1990, making it a relatively young festival, celebrated on the fifteenth day of the first month of the lunar calendar, and at the very end of the Chinese New Year.

Inspired by traditional folklore, this ceremony lasts a whole night, during which thousands of sky lanterns shine over the Pingxi District in Taiwan.

The lanterns are released to ward off evil and let the locals know that the town is safe.

Lantern Images

The main lanterns are always over ten meters tall and they normally reflect Chinese zodiac signs.

Each of them shows up with its own theme music, which makes the entire performance even more magical.

In addition to the main lanterns, there are also hundreds of smaller ones, usually carried by children.

These lanterns portray images of historical characters or a specific theme.

La Tomatina

In Bunol near Valencia, Spain, the biggest tomato fight you will ever see happens every year on the last Wednesday of August.

La Tomatina participants have traditionally been throwing tomatoes at each other purely for entertainment purposes since 1945.

History suggests that the event began by accident by youngsters participating in the Gigantes y Cabezudos festival with enormous figures with big heads.

They grabbed some tomatoes from a nearby stall and threw them at one of the giant figures who accidentally fell down.

How the Tradition Grew

The next year, the same young people returned to the place with their own tomatoes and started a tomato fight.

Nowadays, La Tomatina is so popular that tomatoes are delivered in large trucks to thousands of people from all over the world.
