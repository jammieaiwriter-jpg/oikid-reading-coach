Grammar Check
Grammar 1
Which sentence is passive voice?
The receiver of the action is first.
Grammar 2
Which sentence correctly changes 'My mother cleaned the windows' to passive voice?
Use were plus cleaned.
Grammar 3
The house ___ built in 2012.
Use was + past participle for one house in the past.

混合朗讀/口說 (Mixed Reading and Speaking)
混合朗讀/口說 (Mixed Reading and Speaking) 1
Why was Susan looking for a job?
Say what her parents would stop giving and what she needed to earn.
Reference Answer
Susan was looking for a job because her parents would stop giving her money and she needed to earn her own money.
混合朗讀/口說 (Mixed Reading and Speaking) 2
What happened at the flower shop?
Mention Judy, the surprise, and the parents.
Reference Answer
At the flower shop, Judy surprised Susan with flowers, and their parents helped them open the shop.
混合朗讀/口說 (Mixed Reading and Speaking) 3
Use a number-line word to describe where 0 is.
Use between negative one and one.
Reference Answer
Zero is between negative one and one on a number line.
