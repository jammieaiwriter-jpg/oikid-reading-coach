區塊 3 / 5　③ 文法 A：There be 存在句（＝知洞 GP-11）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
There is + 單數／不可數名詞；There are + 複數名詞。否定：There isn't / There aren't + any...。問句：Is there...? / Are there...?，短答用 Yes, there is. / No, there isn't. / Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.。規則記好直接作答，每題答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 5 / 5　⑤ GEPT 延伸閱讀：A Ranger's Day at the Reserve講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 220 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。這篇短文情境是野生動物保護區的巡護員，跟①區的紀錄片互利夥伴故事不同。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Meet Mei. She works at a wildlife reserve in Africa. A reserve is a big, safe place for wild animals. Mei is a ranger. Her job is to protect the animals every day.

Every morning, Mei drives her truck across the reserve. She looks for lions, zebras, and elephants. She counts each animal and writes the number in her book. This helps her know if all the animals are safe.

Today, Mei sees a young elephant near a tree. Its leg looks hurt. Mei stops the truck and calls her team on the radio. "There is a hurt elephant near the river," she says. "Please come and help."

Soon, another ranger comes with medicine. Together, they check the elephant's leg. It is not too bad. The elephant can still walk, but it needs to rest for a few days. Mei feels happy that the animal will be fine.

After lunch, Mei drives to see a group of zebras by the water. She watches them drink and eat grass. No hunters are near, so the zebras are safe today. Mei writes notes about each animal she sees.

At the end of the day, the sun goes down, and Mei drives back to the office. She is tired, but she loves her job. "I protect these animals because the world needs them," she says with a smile.
