全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Last Monday morning, I woke up and my head hurt a lot. My whole body felt weak, and I had a bad headache too.

My mom came into my room. She touched my head and my neck. "Why do you look so sick?" she asked.

"I do not know," I said. "My head hurts, and my stomach hurts too. Maybe I am sick because of the rain yesterday."

My mom checked my temperature. "You have a fever," she said. "You are sick, so you must stay home today."

She gave me some medicine and a glass of warm water. Then she called the school and sent them a message about my fever.

In the afternoon, my arm, my knee, and my shoulder all felt sore. I did not want to speak or move much.

"Why don't you eat something?" my mom asked. "I am not hungry, so I only want water," I said. She said my illness was not serious.

By evening, I felt a little stronger. My mom said healthy food and good medicine could help my body get well again.

The next day, I felt much better. I put on my coat, wore a mask, and went back to school with a strong, healthy body.

全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Yesterday, my hand and my arm felt sore after gym class.

This morning, I have a headache and a fever, so I feel sick.

My mom checks my head and says, "You are sick. Stay in bed today."

The doctor asks why my knee, my leg, and my neck hurt so much.

I try to explain: my stomach hurts, my shoulder is stiff, and my whole body feels weak.

The doctor gives me some medicine and says I need to wear a mask outside.

Good medicine and a strong body can help a sick person stay healthy again.

My illness is not serious, but the doctor treats it as a normal cold, not something worse.

Maybe I need warm clothes and a thick coat, because the weather in this country is cold.

Good health is important, so I speak with the doctor and promise to rest well.

I get better, and my mom gives me one small bowl of ice cream. I happily wiggle my toe under the warm blanket.

區塊 4 / 6　④ 文法 B：why問句與because/so（＝知洞 GP-34）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
Why 開頭問「為什麼」，回答時用 because 說明原因（I am sick because I got wet in the rain）。so 用來連接「原因，所以結果」（I have a fever, so I stay home）。because 放在原因子句前，so 放在結果子句前，兩者不會同時出現在同一句。規則記好直接作答，答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 6 / 6　⑥ GEPT 延伸閱讀：A Get-Well Visit for Zoe講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 260 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

My friend Zoe did not come to school last Friday. I did not know why, so I called her house after school. Her mother answered the phone and said Zoe was sick.

On Saturday, my mom and I visited Zoe. She was in bed with a warm blanket. Her face looked pale, and her eyes looked tired. She said her head hurt and her whole body felt weak.

Zoe's mother said Zoe had a high fever on Thursday night. She took Zoe to see a doctor the next morning. The doctor checked her arms, her neck, and her stomach carefully.

The doctor said it was not serious, but Zoe needed to rest at home for a few days. He gave Zoe some medicine and told her to drink a lot of warm water.

Zoe said the medicine tasted terrible, but she took it every morning and every night. Her mother also made her a bowl of warm soup and gave her extra blankets to stay warm.

I gave Zoe a card and a small gift. She smiled and said thank you. I told her that our class missed her a lot and everyone hoped she would get better soon.

On Monday morning, Zoe came back to school. She wore a mask and looked much stronger. She said the medicine and the rest really helped her body get well again.

Our teacher was happy to see Zoe. She said health is very important, so we should always rest well and eat good food. Zoe smiled and said she agreed completely.
