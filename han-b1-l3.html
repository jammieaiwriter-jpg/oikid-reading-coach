全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Today our class is at the museum. We are here for a school trip. Many boys and girls are here too. Everyone is excited!

A tour guide is a tall man with a sign. Please be quiet, he says. Follow me, and don't fight in the line.

Now it is time to see the dinosaurs. Look at this dinosaur! It is very big. Can we take pictures? Sure, you can!

Next we walk to the mummy room. A mummy is very old. Please don't touch it. Check the sign for the rules.

We can see a dinosaur or a mummy first, but wait for your turn at the door. Don't hurry, and don't push.

Now we go to the lab. A guy in a white coat works there. There are many test tubes and rocks on the table.

Can we use the test tubes? No, you can't, the guy says. It is not safe. Be careful, and look at the rocks carefully.

Please wash your hands after you touch the rocks. Talk quietly in the lab. At the end, we watch a magic show. This museum is a great place!

區塊 4 / 6　④ 文法 B：助動詞 can（＝知洞 GP-14）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）

【挑戰閱讀】原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內，情境是學校圖書館（跟①區博物館參觀不同）。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

My school has a big library. I go there every Monday after class. The library is quiet, and everyone likes to read there.

Ms. Chen is our librarian. She helps me find good books. Today she shows me a new book about space. I am very happy, and I want to read it now.

There are many rules in the library. You must be quiet, so please don't talk loudly. You can't eat or drink inside. You must put the books back on the shelf when you finish, and you must not run between the desks.

I want to take a book home. Ms. Chen checks my library card first. Then she says, "You can borrow three books. Please return them in two weeks. If you are late, you can't borrow a new book next time."

My friend Nina is also in the library. She can't find her math book. "Is it under the table?" I ask. "No, it isn't," she says. We look together, and after a minute, we find it near the window, behind a big dictionary.

Before we go home, Ms. Chen gives us a small sticker. It says, "I love reading!" Nina and I are very happy about it.

We put the stickers on our bags. We can't wait to come back to the library next week and read more new books.
