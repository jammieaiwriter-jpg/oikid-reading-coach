區塊 1 / 6　① 課文朗讀：Ordering Food with a Delivery App（原創）講義 原創課次對應文本（第一次用外送 app 點餐的家庭故事）
原創課次文本，用本課單字和累積文法寫成（課本課文請看課本跟著念）。一次念一整段，念完做理解題——題目從這篇文字就能作答。
全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Last month, my family downloaded a new food delivery app on our phone. My little brother was excited to try ordering food for the first time.

The app showed a long list of restaurants near our location. There were several choices, from noodles to pizza, so we had a hard time picking one.

My dad tapped on a small chart that showed each restaurant's rating among all the users. He said the rating was a good reason to choose a place quickly.

We finally picked a small noodle shop nearby. The app also gave a discount to new users, so my mom felt happy about the lower price.

While we waited on the couch, my brother kept checking the app again and again. He said he felt a little bored just sitting there doing nothing.

Then some news came on the screen: our driver's location had moved close to our building. My brother got excited again and jumped up from the couch.

When the driver arrived, he wore a green uniform and gave us a friendly smile. My mom thanked him for the fast service and gave him a small tip.

But when we opened the bags at the table, one bowl of soup was missing. My brother looked angry, and my mom looked surprised too.

My dad said he did not mind calling the shop about the mistake. He believed that a small problem like this could happen sometimes, so he stayed calm.

The shop was very helpful and said sorry right away. They sent the missing soup with another driver, and in the end, we all agreed it was a tiring but interesting first try.

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：情緒動詞及分詞形容詞（＝知洞 GP-51）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
情緒動詞加上 -ed 或 -ing 會變成兩種不同意思的形容詞：-ed 形容詞（bored、excited、tired、surprised、interested、scared）用來形容「人」的感受；-ing 形容詞（boring、exciting、tiring、surprising、interesting、scary）用來形容「造成這種感受的事物」。例如：I am bored（我感到無聊）vs. This is boring（這件事很無聊）。答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 4 / 6　④ 文法 B：that 名詞子句（＝知洞 GP-53）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）

Every Friday night, my family walks to the night market near our home. The market opens at six o'clock and stays open until midnight.

There are many small stands selling food, clothes, and toys. My favorite stand sells hot noodles with warm soup.

My little sister likes to play games at the market. She often tries to throw a ring onto a bottle to win a small prize.

Last Friday, we met my uncle at the market. He works at a stand that sells fresh fruit every night.

My uncle gave us a free bag of grapes. He said the grapes were sweet and good for us.

We walked around the market for almost two hours. We looked at many things but did not buy very much.

Near the end of the night, my dad bought a big cup of milk tea for the whole family to share.

We sat on a bench and drank the tea together. It was a simple but happy way to end the week.

On the way home, my sister asked if we could come back next Friday. My mom smiled and said, "Of course we can."

I think the night market is one of the best parts of living in this city. There is always something new to see.
