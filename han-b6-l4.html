原創課次文本，用本課單字和累積文法寫成（課本課文請看課本跟著念）。一次念一整段，念完做理解題——題目從這篇文字就能作答。
全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Every Friday night, my grandfather and I climb up to the roof, where the sky is clear and quiet. He is the person who first taught me the alphabet of the night sky, the way each pattern of stars has its own name.

Grandpa often says that watching the stars reminds him of his youth, when he dreamed about traveling into outer space one day. He always encourages me to keep asking questions, because he believes that curiosity is where wisdom begins.

Last Saturday, we raised a small telescope onto the roof before it got dark outside. Would you mind holding the tripod steady, he asked, while I turned the lens toward the moon slowly and carefully.

My grandfather has an old notebook whose pages are full of star maps that he drew when he was young. It is the notebook in which he wrote down every comet he ever saw, and it still sits on the shelf near his bed.

He told me that the same stars we see tonight were also seen by people who lived long ago, in places far away from here. That thought, he said, is a kind of wisdom that never grows old.

I told him that I want to study astronomy at a university someday, and he smiled and said that dreams like mine keep him feeling young. Every night we sit outside together, and I already know that I will remember these nights forever.

區塊 7 / 7　⑦ GEPT 延伸閱讀：Our School Garden Club講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 240 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內，情境與祖孫觀星課文及四篇文章庫皆不重疊。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思→ ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Every Wednesday, a small group of students meets behind the school. We take care of our garden club there. We started the club last spring. Now more than twenty students join us every week.

At first, we only planted a few rows of plants. We grew tomatoes and carrots. A teacher who grew up in a small village showed us what to do. She showed us how to get the ground ready. Then we put the seeds in.

Taking care of a garden is not easy. We have to clear the grass almost every day. We water the plants well. We do not want the roots to get too wet.

In early fall, we had our first harvest. Everyone was excited. We carried baskets of fresh vegetables back to the classroom. Our teacher helped us wash them before lunch.

We shared some of the vegetables with an older neighbor. She lives near the school gate. She thanked us with a warm smile. She said the tomatoes reminded her of her own garden.

Now our club wants new volunteers. We want to build a small fence around the garden. A fence will keep wild animals away. It will also protect the young plants in winter.

Every member has learned one big lesson. Growing food takes time, and the whole team must work well together. Nobody expected that a small part of the ground could teach us so much.
