全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Last weekend, my family packed our tent and drove two hours to a small campsite near the mountains. My little brother was so excited that he asked us to arrive early.

When we got there, we quickly set up our tent on the soft grass. My dad boiled water for tea while my mom laid out our sleeping bags gently, one by one, and smiled at us.

In the afternoon, my brother wanted to climb a small hill behind the campsite. He hopped over the wet rocks near a stream, and I had to count his steps carefully so he would not fall.

My sister collected leaves and small stones to write about in her diary. She tied a piece of string around her favorite leaf so it would not blow away in the wind.

That night, my cousin danced so crazily around the fire that everyone called her the dancing queen of the camp. We clapped and cheered loudly until our hands were tired.

Later, we lay on the grass under the bright moon and looked up at the starry sky. My dad taught me the shape of a few star patterns, and my brother tried to spell their names out loud.

Before we packed up our socks and shoes the next morning, my brother laughed so hard that he could not shape his last marshmallow into a star.

On the way home, everyone agreed that this camping trip went really well, and my little brother wrote one word in his diary: "perfect."

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：情狀副詞、程度副詞與焦點副詞（＝知洞 GP-31）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
情狀副詞描述動作「怎麼做」（quickly、gently、loudly、crazily、well、hard），程度副詞加強語氣（very、really、so、too），焦點副詞聚焦在句中特定成分上（only、even、just）。三種放在一起練，答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 4 / 6　④ 文法 B：形容詞&副詞比較級(最高級)進階面向（＝知洞 GP-45）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
進階面向說明：康軒 B4-L2 的文法區已把 GP-45（形容詞&副詞比較級／最高級）用在最基礎的面向——規則變化的形容詞 -er/-est（taller/tallest）、較長形容詞的 more/most，以及 good/bad 這類不規則形態的基本比較句型（本次動工時 specs/ 目錄下尚未建立 kang-b4-l2.spec.json 這份檔案，查無現成 L2 spec 可直接核對，此推斷依據 GP-44「比較級(原級&比較級)」已在 B4-L1 教過、GP-45 在 B4-L2 才第一次出現、以及康軒教材同一文法點在同冊初次出現時多半先教規則變化基本句型的通例）。為避免本課（L4）跟 L2 撞題型，本區改取「進階面向」：(1) 副詞的比較級/最高級（quickly→more quickly/most quickly，而非形容詞），(2) 比較級前加強修飾語（much/a lot/even + 比較級），(3) one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞句型，(4) the + 比較級, the + 比較級（越..越..）雙重比較句型。全部是 L2 基礎規則變化沒有涵蓋到的用法。

先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Every spring, our school holds a talent show in the gym. This year, I decided to join for the first time and play the piano in front of my classmates and teachers.

For three weeks, I practiced every night after dinner. My little sister always sat next to me and clapped when I finished a song, even when I made small mistakes.

On the day of the show, I felt very nervous. My hands were cold, and my heart was beating fast while I waited behind the curtain.

When the teacher called my name, I walked out slowly and sat down at the piano. I took a deep breath and looked for my family in the front row.

I started to play, and after the first few notes, my nervous feeling slowly disappeared. The music felt calm and familiar, just like it did at home.

When I finished, everyone in the gym began to clap and cheer loudly. My principal smiled and said my song was one of the best in the whole show.

After the show, my friends told me they did not know I had so much talent for music. My little sister said she wants to learn an instrument too.

That night, I wrote in my diary that trying something new, even when it feels a little scary, can turn into a proud and happy memory.
