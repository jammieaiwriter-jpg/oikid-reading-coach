全文（點任一字查中文意思）

My family makes a new chore chart every week. On Monday, my dad is washing the car. My mom is cooking lunch, and we are cleaning our rooms. Everybody has a job this week.

On Tuesday and Wednesday, we take turns. My sister is feeding our dog at seven a.m. I am setting the table at noon. My mom is washing the dishes at eight p.m.

Thursday is busy. Mom has a meeting at six o'clock, so Dad is preparing dinner alone. The sun is still shining outside. I say, "I can help you, Dad!" He smiles and says thanks.

Friday is Mom's birthday! This year it falls on May 8th. We are not doing chores that night. Everyone is planning a small party, and Dad is buying a cake.

Our family has a big cleaning day every year, on Saturday, June 3rd. This Saturday, we are painting the old fence together. Later, we join our neighbors for basketball. City life is busy, but weekends can be fun.

Sunday is our rest day. Tonight we are not working; we are chatting on the sofa. Everybody rises late on Sunday morning. Next week, our chart starts again on Monday!

Everybody in my class is friendly. Everyone wants to join our study group after school. Our city has many parks for us to meet.

The sun begins to rise in the morning and shine over the city. Tonight, the sky is clear, and we can see many stars.

區塊 6 / 6　⑥ GEPT 延伸閱讀：A Video Call Across the World講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內，情境跟課文（家事輪值表）不同——這次是跨時區的家庭視訊通話。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Mia lives in Taipei. Her cousin Leo lives in New York. The two cities are far away from each other, but the cousins talk every week on a video call.

Time is different in the two places. When it is morning in Taipei, it is still night in New York. So they must plan their call time with care.

This Sunday, Mia is sitting at her desk at nine in the morning. She is holding her tablet and waiting for the call. At the same moment, Leo is eating his dinner. For him, it is eight in the evening.

"Hi, Mia! Can you hear me?" Leo asks with a smile. "Yes, I can hear you well!" Mia says. They are talking about school and friends. Leo is telling her about his new soccer team, and Mia is telling him about her art class.

Then Mia shows Leo a photo of her dog. Leo shows her a photo of his new bike. They both laugh and talk for almost an hour.

Before they say goodbye, they check their plans for next week. Their next call will be on Saturday. For Leo, it will be Saturday afternoon. For Mia, it will be early Sunday morning.

A video call is nice, but it is not the same as a real hug. Still, it helps a family feel close. Every week, Mia and Leo wait for this special hour together.
