Wrong-Answer Practice
Likely confusion: worker and action
Which word names the person who fixes cars?
Choose the worker, not the action or thing.
Likely confusion: negative and positive
Which number is positive?
Positive numbers are more than zero.
Likely confusion: compound words
Which is made from tooth and brush?
Put the two smaller words together.

Do your parents work? What do they do?
Say one parent job and what the person does.
Reference Answer
Yes, my parents work. My dad designs things, and my mom helps our family at home.
混合朗讀/口說 (Mixed Reading and Speaking) 3
Which is more important, doing what you want or doing a job many people do?
Choose one idea and give a reason.
Reference Answer
Doing what I want is more important because I can work hard and enjoy helping people.
