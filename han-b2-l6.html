全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Last weekend, my family took a trip to an old town. We love to travel together every year, but this time we did not fly there — we drove there instead.

My little sister was so excited about the trip at her age. She did not sleep the whole way, and Dad had to drive for three hours.

On the way, we passed a small airport and saw a plane fly by. We also passed a train station, but we just kept driving. We did not stop at either place.

When we got to the old town, we rented bikes. We rode past a famous temple with a golden roof. People built it long ago, and it was almost as tall as our school building.

Besides the temple, the old town has a busy morning market. My mom bought fresh fruit, and I bought a small wooden fork at a flea market stall.

After lunch at a small restaurant, we visited a bookstore and a big department store. My sister wanted to buy something, but she lost her money there.

She cried and wanted to hide because she felt bad. “Did you lose it on the bike?” Mom asked. “I do not know why,” my sister said sadly.

We did not find the money, but Dad gave her some for a small gift. We took a short rest, then went to an old theater and saw the sights from the roof.

We did not stay at a hotel that night; we drove home instead. My sister said, “I want to remember this story forever!” It was a cool trip, and we will never forget it.

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：過去式疑問句（＝知洞 GP-29）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
不規則動詞的過去式疑問句：Did + 主詞 + 動詞原形？動詞一律回到原形，不能用過去式（不是 Did you bought，而是 Did you buy）。規則記好直接作答，每題答完把整句唱出來。

區塊 4 / 6　④ 文法 B：過去式否定句（＝知洞 GP-29）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）

Last night, I woke up at midnight. I heard a strange noise outside my window. It sounded like someone was walking on the roof. My heart beat fast, and I did not move.

I did not want to wake my parents, so I got out of bed alone. I walked to the window very slowly. The noise stopped, but then it started again. It was louder this time.

I picked up my flashlight and opened my door a little. The hallway was dark and quiet. I took a deep breath and told myself, "Don't be afraid. It's probably nothing."

I walked to the kitchen because the noise seemed to come from there. When I turned on the light, I saw a small cat near the trash can! It jumped when it saw me.

The cat ran to the back door and pushed it open with its paw. Someone did not close the door well that night. The cat had come inside to find food.

I laughed and felt silly. The "monster" was just a hungry cat from next door! I gave it some milk, and it drank all of it happily.

I opened the back door again, and the cat walked outside. It looked back at me once before it ran into the dark garden.

The next morning, I told my family the whole story at breakfast. My little brother did not believe me at first, but then he saw the empty milk bowl. We all laughed together.

Now, every night before I sleep, I check the back door twice. I don't want another cat to visit at midnight and give me such a big scare again!
