區塊 1 / 6　① 課文朗讀：Sunshine Orchard（原創）講義 原創課次對應文本（南臺灣家族果園）
原創課次文本，用本課單字和文法（授與動詞、感官動詞）寫成（課本課文請看課本跟著念）。一次念一整段，念完做理解題——題目從這篇文字就能作答。
全文（點任一字查中文意思）

My grandfather is the leader of Sunshine Orchard, a small fruit farm in southern Taiwan. The climate here is warm and wet, so grapes, mangoes, papayas, and guavas grow very well all year.

Every morning, I watch the workers pick ripe peaches and pears in the field. My grandfather always sends the best fruit to the local supermarket before nine o'clock.

Grandfather is a skillful farmer. He taught me how to plant a small seed and wait for it to grow into a strong tree. Several workers help him every day.

Last year, Grandfather wanted to create something new. He mixed watermelon, strawberry, and lemon with a little sugar and water. He made a fresh juice for the whole family.

We all tasted it and loved it! I could hear my little sister say, This juice is excellent! My mouth was happy all afternoon.

Grandfather wanted to improve the juice, so he tried it many times. Soon, the juice became very popular. He successfully sold it to a big supermarket in the city.

Now, people call our farm a small paradise. Grandfather even sends boxes of fruit and juice to my aunt abroad in America. She says the juice tastes like home.

Every quarter, Grandfather plants new seeds so the orchard never stops growing. I feel proud when I watch him working in the field. I want to be a skillful farmer like him one day.

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：授與動詞（＝知洞 GP-42）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
授與動詞（give 類）後面可以接兩個受詞：「動詞＋人＋物」（give sb sth）；也可以改寫成「動詞＋物＋to/for＋人」。give、send、show、tell、teach、pass 用 to；buy、make、cook、find 用 for。規則記好直接作答，每題答完把整句唸出來。

先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

My name is Owen. Last month, my family and I visited my uncle's small shop near the harbor. My uncle sells fresh fruit juice there every day.

At the shop, my uncle showed me how he makes juice. He picks ripe mangoes, papayas, and lemons, then mixes them with a little sugar and water.

A worker there told us a fact. Many small shops like this one send their fruit juice to restaurants all over the city every week.

On weekends, many customers wait in a line outside the small shop. They come from every part of the city just to try his sweet, fresh juice.

We watched my uncle work quickly and carefully. He is a skillful worker, and his juice tastes excellent. That is why the shop is so popular.

My uncle said his shop was once very quiet, like a paradise with no customers at all. He worked hard for several years to improve his recipe.

My uncle told us he wants to open a second juice shop next year. He hopes more people around the city can enjoy his excellent juice every day.

Before we left, my uncle gave us a free cup of juice. He said, I hope you visit again soon. I want to come back and help him next time.
