全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Last Saturday, my family visited the weekend market near our apartment. A shopkeeper stood behind a small stall, calling out a big promotion on fresh fruit.

He held up a sample lemon and said, "Buy two kilograms of peaches, and I will give you a free bag of tomatoes!" The deal sounded amazing.

My mom picked a pear and a few peaches. The shopkeeper placed them above a small metal scale, and the number appeared: exactly one kilogram.

But my little brother, who studies weight and height at school, noticed something strange below the scale's bottom.

There was a thin gray marker line and a tiny pin stuck into the wood — a hidden weight that made the total look higher than it really was.

"Wait," my brother said. "Therefore, this scale shows more grams than the fruit actually weighs. That's not fair to customers."

The shopkeeper, whose face turned red, tried to explain that the pin was just holding a torn paper sign in place with superglue and paste.

My dad, who understood the trick right away, asked the shopkeeper to weigh the fruit again on a different scale.

This time, the level read only eight hundred grams, not one kilogram — a difference of about two hundred grams, or nearly half a pound.

The shopkeeper apologized and gave us a fair price, plus a real free sample. My brother said, "Never waste your money — always check what a promotion really means."

On the way home, my dad opened his wallet and said the shopkeeper's trick made him think twice about every promotion sign he sees now. We agreed to always check numbers, not just believe a friendly smile.

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：受格關係代名詞 who／that（可省略）（＝知洞 GP-56・進階面向一）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
GP-56（關係代名詞）版內分工：L5（基礎）教主格關係代名詞——who/which/that 在子句中當主詞、後面直接接動詞，介紹限定關係子句的基本概念；本課（L6）接續這個分工，教進階面向一：受格關係代名詞——who/that（人，作受詞）、that（事物，作受詞），受格關代後面接的是「主詞+動詞」而不是直接接動詞（這是和 L5 主格題型最大的分辨點），而且受格關代常常可以省略，句子拿掉它一樣完整、一樣文法正確。刻意避開 L5 已經考過的『關代+動詞』主格題型，兩課互不重疊。答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 4 / 6　④ 文法 B：所有格 whose／介系詞＋關係代名詞（＝知洞 GP-56・進階面向二）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
GP-56 進階面向二：whose 是所有格關係代名詞，後面直接接名詞，人和事物都可以用（the shopkeeper whose scale...／the stall whose roof...）；介系詞＋關係代名詞（in which／with whom／under which）是把原本放在句尾的介系詞移到關代前面的正式寫法，比『that/which...介系詞放句尾』更正式。這兩點都是 L5 主格關代沒教過的進階用法，和 gramA 的受格關代／省略也是不同面向，三塊互不重疊。答完把整句唸出來。

【挑戰閱讀】約 250 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。按「換一篇」可在文章庫四篇（新生訓練日敘事／班級大掃除說明文／午休圖書室告示／校刊編輯室書信）之間輪替。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

On my first day at my new school, we had an orientation day for all new students. I was very nervous as I walked through the front gate with my parents.

A teacher named Mr. Chen was standing by the entrance with a big smile. He gave every new student a small map of the school and a welcome card.

First, we walked around the campus in small groups. Our group leader showed us the library, the gym, and the science labs on the second floor.

In the library, a librarian explained how to borrow books and how many books we could keep at one time. I put the rules down in my notebook.

After the tour, we went to the auditorium for a short speech from the principal. She asked us to try new clubs and make new friends this year.

Then some older students sang a welcome song for us. Everyone smiled and clapped, and I finally started to feel less nervous.

At lunch, I ate with two boys from my new class. We talked about our favorite classes and shared our lunch boxes.

In the afternoon, each class picked a class leader and worked together to write class rules on a big piece of paper.

Before we went home, Mr. Chen reminded us to bring our textbooks and a pencil case on the first real day of class.

I walked out of the school gate feeling excited instead of nervous. I could not wait to start learning with my new classmates.
