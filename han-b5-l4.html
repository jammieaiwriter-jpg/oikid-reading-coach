區塊 1 / 6　① 課文朗讀：A Smart Kind of Day（原創）講義 原創課次對應文本（現代科技融入生活情境）
原創課次文本，用本課單字和文法（間接問句、間接引述、wh- 不定詞片語）寫成（課本課文請看課本跟著念）。一次念一整段，念完做理解題——題目從這篇文字就能作答。
全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Every morning, a small speaker on our kitchen counter listens for its wake word. I say the wake word quietly, and the voice assistant turns on our lamp and plays soft music while I get ready for school.

At breakfast, my mom asks whether the fridge's small screen shows any error today. My dad checks the screen and says everything looks fine, so we do not need to call anyone about the machine.

At school, our teacher still uses the blackboard and chalk sometimes, but she also shows us a modern screen at the front. She asks us whether we know how to upload our homework files to the class cloud folder.

Before our quiz, my friend leans over and asks me what to do if the screen shows an error message. I tell her to enter her code again slowly and connect to the school network first.

After school, my mom sends me to the corner shop with a shopping list. The shopkeeper helps me find a dozen eggs and a small jar of honey, and I pay with the coins from my wallet.

That evening, my dad gets an e-mail about our building's smart gate. He enters a special code on his cellphone, and a small machine unlocks the gate so our car key is not even needed anymore.

After dinner, we watch a short program about new VR products. The host explains that this kind of modern technology can shape the way people learn, work, and play almost everywhere in the world.

Suddenly, we hear our neighbor's cat crying outside the gate. My dad presses a button on his phone, and the gate opens through the app, so he can save the cat before it gets hurt.

Before I go to bed, I ask the voice assistant whether tomorrow's weather will be sunny. It answers right away, and I smile, thinking about how many small ways modern machines now help my whole family every day.

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A1：間接問句與間接引述（＝知洞 GP-54）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
wonder/know/tell/ask 等動詞後面接一個「問句」時，問句要變回「敘述句語序」（主詞在助動詞前面），不能維持原本問句的倒裝；yes/no 問題用 whether/if 開頭。每題答完把整句唸出來。

先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Every summer, I stay at my grandpa's house for a week. This Saturday, my bike chain falls off, so I ask him for help. He smiles and pulls a heavy wooden box out from under the stairs.

The box is old and a little dusty, but every tool inside has its own place. Grandpa says his own father gave him this toolbox many years ago, when he was still a young man.

I ask him how he learned to fix so many things by himself. He explains that nobody taught him with a manual. He just watched his father work and tried the same steps again and again.

While he fixes my chain, he asks me whether I want to learn too. I nod, and he shows me which tool to pick up first and how to hold it without hurting my fingers.

Inside the box, I find a small metal key that does not match any lock I know. I wonder what it is for, so I ask Grandpa quietly what he keeps it for all these years.

He laughs and explains that the key once opened his very first bicycle lock, from when he was ten years old. He never sells that bicycle, and he never throws the key away either.

After my chain is fixed, we ride our bikes slowly around the yard together. The evening light turns gold, and I feel proud that I finally know how to fix a bike chain myself.

That night, I ask Grandpa if I can keep one small screwdriver from the box. He says yes, and I understand then that this toolbox holds more than tools. It holds our whole family's story.
