區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：現在進行式（＝知洞 GP-10）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）

區塊 4 / 6　④ 文法 B：What／Who／Where 問句（＝知洞 GP-06）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
What 問事物／做什麼，Who 問人，Where 問地方，常跟現在進行式搭配，例如 What is he doing? / Who is feeding the puppy? / Where are you? 直接作答，答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 6 / 6　⑦ GEPT 延伸閱讀：A Busy Morning at the Breakfast Shop講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 200 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

It is Saturday morning. I am at my uncle's breakfast shop. He is very busy, so I am his little helper today. I am excited!

The shop opens at six. My uncle is making egg pancakes and milk tea. The eggs come from a farm in the country. They are big and good.

Many people are waiting in line. A tall man wants two boxes of bread. A little girl is drinking milk with her mom.

A boy from my school is here, too. He is buying breakfast for his family. "Are you working here?" he asks. "Yes! Come again!" I say.

I am not fast, but I am careful. I give people their food and say thank you. My uncle is smiling at me.

At ten, an old woman comes in. She is our neighbor. She gives us a small gift. It is a box of cookies from her kitchen!

At noon, the shop is quiet. My uncle makes a big breakfast just for me. This busy morning is hard work, but I love it. Next Saturday, I want to help again.
