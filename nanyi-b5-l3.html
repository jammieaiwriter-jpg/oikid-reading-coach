區塊 1 / 6　① 課文朗讀：The Fact-Check Club（原創）講義 原創課次對應文本（社團核實假新聞）
原創課次文本，用本課單字和文法（GP-51 情緒動詞及分詞形容詞、GP-50 附加問句）寫成（課本課文請看課本跟著念）。一次念一整段，念完做理解題——題目從這篇文字就能作答。
全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Last month, our English teacher started a new after-school club. She said its job was simple: read online news carefully and teach members how to fact-check every headline before believing it.

At first, the club sounded boring, and I felt bored with little interest. But after the first meeting, I felt excited about a crazy headline: a strange habit made a purple rose grow on a boy's finger.

The story even had a photo of a handsome man in a white coat, holding a report. It said the rose was exactly ten centimeters long, about four inches.

We decided to dig deeper. Using a few keywords, we searched trusted websites for real facts. Below the headline, a fact-check report said the whole story was fake.

Someone had posted it as a joke to fool people on April 1st. Thank God we did not believe it or share it with our friends online.

The next headline was about health. It said one piece of magic medicine could cure a fever and a bad headache overnight, without seeing a real doctor.

Our leader's tip was simple: never drop your guard around medical news. She taught us that a heavy topic like health needs careful, honest fact-checking every single time.

Then a member stubbed her toe rushing to show us a purple cat photo. She said it must be fake, just a color filter, but the story was actually true.

A real news report said a slim, tall man weighs about ninety kilograms, which is close to two hundred pounds. That fact surprised everyone in our small club.

Our leader warned that some fake websites try to steal personal information, so we should always use safe tools. This club taught me a valuable lesson, didn't it?

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：情緒動詞及分詞形容詞（＝知洞 GP-51）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
GP-51 情緒動詞及分詞形容詞本課聚焦：人＝Ved（感到……的，被動、感受者），物/事＝Ving（令人……的，主動、原因）。例：I am bored（我感到無聊）vs. It is boring（它很無聊）。常見對：bored/boring、excited/exciting、interested/interesting、tired/tiring、surprised/surprising。答完把整句唸出來。

Every month, I visit my aunt in another town by bus, and I always change buses at a big transfer station on the way. The station is busy, and at first it feels hard to understand.

Inside, there are many gates with numbers, and a big screen shows the bus times. My bus usually leaves from gate twelve, but the gates are easy to mix up.

Once, I could not find gate twelve, so I asked a worker in a blue uniform for help. She points down a long hall and says, Walk straight, then turn right.

I hurry down the hall, past a small shop that sells drinks and snacks. My heart beats fast because the bus leaves in only five minutes.

When I finally reach gate twelve, the driver almost closes the door. I wave my hand and call out, and he waits for me in a kind way.

I sit down, very tired, and watch the station grow smaller through the window. I am happy, and I almost forget to thank the driver.

Now I always leave home early when I have to change buses. That trip teaches me that a big station can feel hard to understand, but a friendly worker can help so much.
