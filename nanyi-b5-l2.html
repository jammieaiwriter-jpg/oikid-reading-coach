全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Every year, our school holds Sports Day in November. This year, the sky was clear and blue, and everyone felt excited before the big race began.

My PE teacher told us the 100-meter race would start soon. I felt a little sore from morning practice, but I did some stretches near the sidewalk.

When the flag was raised, all the runners started to run. The crowd let out a loud scream, and I ran as fast as I could down the track.

I was so thirsty by the finish line, but I kept running. Above the crowd, a small airplane pulled a colorful banner through the sky.

I won second place! A shiny medal was placed around my neck by my PE teacher, and I was given a small prize too.

After the race, I could finally rest. My friend played the drum in the school band, and another classmate played the piano for the closing show.

In the afternoon, some students flew kites near the field, and a few boys played badminton by the wall. Everyone said the whole day was truly exciting.

At night, fireworks lit up the sky above our school. It was almost one mile from my home, but I could still see the bright lights from my window.

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：被動語態基本式（＝知洞 GP-52 面向一）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
被動語態的基本公式：be動詞（am/is/are/was/were）＋過去分詞（p.p.），主詞是「被做動作的人事物」，不是做動作的人。現在式用 am/is/are，過去式用 was/were。規則記好直接作答，每題答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 4 / 6　④ 文法 B：被動語態的否定句、疑問句與 by（＝知洞 GP-52 面向二）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）

【挑戰閱讀】約 200 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內，情境與課文（校園運動會）無關，是獨立情境（撿到錢包歸還失主）。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

My name is Ryan. Last Saturday, I was walking home from the park when I saw something small on the sidewalk. It was a brown wallet, and it looked quite old.

I picked up the wallet and opened it. Inside, I found some money and a photo of a family. I did not know whose wallet it was, but I wanted to find the owner.

I walked to the police station near my house. A police officer took the wallet and thanked me for being so honest. She said many people would keep the money and never bring it back.

Two days later, the police officer called my mother. She said the owner of the wallet was very happy and wanted to meet me. His name was Mr. Chen, and he lived close to the park.

Mr. Chen came to my house with a small gift. He said the money in the wallet was for his daughter, who was sick and needed medicine. He was very glad that I did not take it.

My mother told me that being honest is more important than money. I felt proud of myself, and I will always try to do the right thing when I find something that is not mine.
