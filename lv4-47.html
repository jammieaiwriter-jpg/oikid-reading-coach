Reading Passage Shadowing
Famous Landmark

This tall building is Taipei 101, it is the most famous landmark in Taipei.

It was the tallest building in the world from 2004 to 2010.

Busy Streets

There are a lot of people walking on Hanzhong street in Ximending.

A street is a small road within a town or city.

Streets may have houses or shops on one or both sides.

There are many busy streets in Taipei.

Traffic Jam

There is a lot of traffic in Taipei, especially during rush hours.

A traffic jam is when too many vehicles are too close together and causing traffic to move very slowly.

It is almost impossible to move because traffic is at a standstill and drivers sit and wait in their cars for a long period of time.

Intersections and Highways

Here are some cars waiting to cross the intersection.

An intersection is a place where roads or streets meet.

Intersections can have a lot of traffic.

A highway is a big road that connects places like cities or towns to different places in the city.

People can go fast on the highway because there are no traffic lights.

Population and Capital

Population means the number of people living in one place.

Taipei has a large population.

Most capital cities have a large population.

Do you know your city's population?

Taipei is a big city in its population.

It is the capital of Taiwan and is located in northern Taiwan.

Important City

Taipei is a big and important city in Taiwan.

It also has the highest density of population.

Taipei is the heart of Taiwanese politics, economics, and culture.

Weather and Sight Words

Taipei has a humid subtropical climate.

Summers in Taipei are long, hot, and humid, and sometimes have heavy rainstorms and typhoons.

The winters are short, generally warm, and very foggy with slight rains.

Nights in Taipei are as beautiful as mornings.
