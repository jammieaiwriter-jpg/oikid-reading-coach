Reading Passage Shadowing
Mayor’s Lunch

A free curry lunch for five thousand city workers and school children was hosted by the mayor of London recently.

The main purpose of the event was to show people how to use food that would otherwise have been thrown out because of its irregular shape, size, or color.

How Big the Problem Is

In countries that have a limited number of fridges or an unreliable electricity supply, large volumes of milk, cheese and yogurt go down the drain before people even have a chance to purchase it.

In UK households, 7.2 million tons of food and drinks that could have been consumed are thrown away.

This is the equivalent of throwing away about 80 dollars worth of food for each household.

Idea One: Keep Apples Longer

We can help the situation by making the most of our food.

Apples are being chucked out by the millions every day.

By loosely wrapping them and storing them in the fridge, they can last up to two weeks longer.

Even if they are bruised, put them in a pie, a crumble or a smoothie and they taste just as good.

Idea Two and Three

You can put baked beans in the freezer within two days of opening.

When you want to eat them, defrost and microwave them until piping hot.

Potato peelings can also be used.

Sprinkle some salt and pepper on them and pop them into the oven to make delicious crisps.
