Family Game Night

短文

Tonight is a special night for Coco's family. It is family game night. Coco is a junior high school student. She is young, but she is really smart. Her cousin Leo is only in elementary school. He is small and very funny.

Who is at family game night? Coco's uncle is there. His name is Ray. He is a police officer. He is tall and handsome. Coco's aunt is there too. Her name is Mia. She is a writer. Her new book is about a young singer.

Where is Coco's mom? She is in the kitchen. Coco's mom is a housewife. She is busy, but she is happy. Coco's dad is an office worker. He is tired after work, but family game night makes him happy too.

What is the first game tonight? It is a family quiz. Each question is about the family. Who is a police officer? Uncle Ray! Who is a writer? Aunt Mia! Coco's baby cousin is quiet in her chair. She is only one year old.

The next game is a word game. Leo is really good at the game. His answer is always funny. Coco's grandma is a big fan of the word game too. She is old, but her mind is quick. Everyone is happy and loud.

Why is family game night so special? It is not about a winner. It is about family time together. Coco's family is small, but it is very dear to her. At the end, everyone is happy. Family game night is really great.

Where Do House Mice Live?

短文

Mice are really small animals. A mouse's body is tiny, but its tail is quite long. Its fur can be gray or brown.

House mice like to live near people. This is because a house has warm rooms and enough food. The kitchen is a mouse's favorite room.

Where does a mouse hide inside a house? It can hide behind the sofa, or in a small hole in the wall. It can even hide inside a kitchen cabinet.

A mouse's nose and whiskers are really useful. They help the mouse smell food from far away. A mouse can smell a cookie between two boxes!

Mice are quick and quiet. This helps them stay safe. A cat is a big danger for a mouse, so a mouse always looks for a safe, small space.

Mice are also clever. If a hole above the floor is too small, a mouse can still get inside. Its body is soft and can go through a tiny crack.

House mice are not really dangerous animals, but people do not want them in the house. That is why people check for cracks near the wall and the floor.

House mice are tiny, quick, and clever. What a special animal a house mouse is!

How Museums Keep Dinosaur Bones Safe

短文

A dinosaur skeleton is really old. Some dinosaur bones are millions of years old! That is why museums use many rules to keep them safe.

First, scientists dig up the bones very carefully. They use small brushes, not big tools. This helps them not to break the old bones.

Next, the bones go to a lab. In the lab, workers clean each bone and check it carefully. They use a test tube to test small rock pieces near the bones too.

At the museum, the dinosaur skeleton is inside a big glass case. This keeps the bones safe from dust and from people's hands.

There is always a sign near the skeleton. It says, "Please do not touch." This rule helps protect the old bones.

The light in the room is not too bright. Bright light is not good for old bones. The room's temperature is also carefully controlled.

A museum guide can tell visitors many facts about the dinosaur. Visitors can take a photo, but they must follow the museum's safety rules.

Now you know why a museum dinosaur skeleton looks so amazing. What a lot of careful work it takes to keep it safe!

A Year of Holidays

短文

A year has twelve months, and many of them have a special holiday. Each holiday has its own story and its own way to celebrate.

January is the first month. On January first, many families are up before midnight on New Year's Eve. When the clock rings, everyone says a wish for a lucky new year.

Some families also celebrate a big holiday around February. It is an important time for family dinner and for giving gifts to children.

Around April, spring is warm, and some families visit family graves before summer comes. It is a quiet, important day for many families.

Around November, some countries celebrate Thanksgiving. Families are thankful, and dinner often has a big turkey leg on the table.

December is the last month. Around Christmas, people decorate a tree with lights. Children are excited, and they are a little nervous too, waiting for a gift.

On New Year's Eve, the last day of December, families gather together again. They count down to midnight and say goodbye to the old year.

From January to December, a year is full of holidays. Each one gives families a reason to be together. A year of holidays is really a year of family time.

The Oxpecker and Its Animal Friends

短文

In the wild, there is a small bird called the oxpecker. This bird has a really special job.

There are many bugs living in the coat of a zebra or a horse. These bugs are not healthy for the big animal.

The oxpecker likes to eat these bugs. So, it sits on the back of a zebra and helps clean its coat.

There is also a big herd of animals in the wild, like a group of zebras or horses. The oxpecker can move from one animal to another and help the whole herd.

Experts say this is a great example of two animals helping each other. The zebra gets a clean coat, and the oxpecker gets food.

There is one more example. If danger is near, like a lion or a tiger, the oxpecker makes a loud sound. This sound can help the zebra know to run.

Luckily for the zebra, the oxpecker is always around. It never leaves its animal friend for long.

So, the next time you see a picture of a zebra, look for a small bird on its back. What a great animal friendship it is!
