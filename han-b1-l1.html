區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：be 動詞（＝知洞 GP-01）講義 原創題

區塊 6 / 6　⑥ GEPT 延伸閱讀：A Letter to My Pen Pal講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 200 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級範圍內。情境是筆友通信介紹家人，跟①區「翻相簿」不同。流程：①先整篇讀過一遍，生字用上下文猜 → ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂 → ③生字補充 → ④最後逐段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Dear Nina,

Hi! My name is Mimi, and I am your new pen pal from Taiwan. Thank you so much for your first letter. I am really happy to write to you today.

Let me tell you about my family. My family is not big, but we are very happy together. My father is an office worker, and he works in the city every day.

My mother is a housewife. She stays at home, and she is really kind to all of us.

I have a big brother and a little sister. My brother is a junior high school student, just like me. My little sister is still in elementary school. She is a happy and cheerful girl, and she likes to sing at home.

I also have grandparents. My grandfather is a writer. He writes books about our small town, and I am proud of him. My grandmother is very dear to me. She tells me nice old stories every night before bed.

My aunt and uncle live near our house. My aunt is a singer, and my uncle is a police officer. Their daughter is my cousin. She is the same age as me, so we are like twins! We are classmates at school, too.

What is your family like, Nina? Are you the only child, or do you have brothers and sisters, too? What are their jobs? I really want to know.

Please write back soon. I am waiting for your next letter!

Your new friend,

Mimi
