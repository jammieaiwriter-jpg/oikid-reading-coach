原創課次文本，用本課文法（天氣的表達方式）與累積文法寫成（課本課文請看課本跟著念）。一次念一整段，念完做理解題——題目從這篇文字就能作答。
全文（點任一字查中文意思）

This Friday, Mr. Lin turns on the TV to watch the weather report. He wants to know about the weather this weekend because his family wants to go hiking near the sea.

"What's the weather like on Saturday?" his daughter Lily asks. The weather reporter says, "It's sunny and warm on Saturday, but it's cloudy on Sunday morning."

"Does it rain a lot in this season?" Lily's brother Tim asks. Mr. Lin answers, "It rains a little in spring, but it gets very windy near the sea in autumn."

On Saturday morning, the sky is blue and sunny. The family walks along the beach and looks at small fishing boats near the water. A fisherman waves and says hello to them.

In the afternoon, dark clouds come from the sea, and the sky becomes dark. "How's the weather now?" Tim asks. Mr. Lin says, "It's getting cloudy, and I think a heavy shower is coming soon."

Lily feels afraid of the dark sky and heavy rain. "Is it dangerous to stay near the sea now?" she asks. "Don't worry. It's safe here. Let's walk back to the car," Mr. Lin says.

Soon, a heavy shower starts, and everyone gets a little wet. They open their umbrella and run to the car. "That was a common summer shower," Mr. Lin says. "The rain in this season is heavy but short."

The next morning, the weather is sunny again. The family visits the same beach and helps pick up plastic bottles on the sand.

"We must protect nature and keep the sea and sky clean," Mr. Lin says. Lily says she wants to be a nature reporter someday.

全文（點任一字查中文意思）

The weather report says it is windy and cloudy this weekend, so people need a coat.

In autumn and fall, the sky often becomes dark before a heavy rain shower.

It is common to feel afraid of loud thunder, but a good umbrella can keep you dry in a wet season.

A fisherman must protect his boat and stay safe when the sea level rises above the dock.

Some sailors live far from land, and they must fix their boats before a big storm.

There is danger in the air when a snowy winter storm comes quickly.

My little sister cries when it is cold, but she does not cry in warm spring or hot summer weather.

I forget my umbrella almost every rainy day, and I often build a small shelter with old boxes.

The sunny sky today is a nice change from yesterday's cloudy morning.

We should protect nature and enjoy the fresh air on a sunny day.

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：天氣問答句型 What's the weather like? / How's the weather?（＝知洞 GP-25 上半）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
問「天氣如何」有兩種常見問法：What's the weather like...? 和 How's the weather...?，回答都用 It's + 天氣形容詞（sunny/rainy/cloudy/windy/snowy/wet…）。規則記好直接作答，每題答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 4 / 6　④ 文法 B：天氣的動詞敘述句型 It rains/snows/gets...（＝知洞 GP-25 下半）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
天氣也可以用動詞直接描述：It rains（下雨）、It snows（下雪）、It showers（陣雨）、It gets + 形容詞（天氣變……）。現在式描述習慣性天氣，現在進行式描述正在發生的天氣，過去式描述以前發生過的天氣。答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 6 / 6　⑥ GEPT 延伸閱讀：A Fisherman's Morning Weather Check講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 230 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

A fisherman named Hank lives in a small village near the sea. Every morning before he goes out on his boat, he always checks the weather report. He knows that the sea can become dangerous very fast.

"What's the weather like today?" his daughter Mei asks him at breakfast. "It's sunny now, but the report says strong wind is coming from the north this afternoon," Hank answers.

In this fishing village, the weather changes with the seasons. It is often windy in autumn, rainy in summer, and calm and sunny in spring. Winter brings cold air and, sometimes, a little snow on the far hills.

Hank says every fisherman should check three things before he leaves: the wind, the clouds, and the sea level near the shore. If the sky looks too dark, he will not go out that day.

One rainy morning, Hank saw heavy black clouds and felt the cold, wet air. He stayed home and fixed his nets.

"Are you afraid of the storm?" Mei asked. "No," he said, "but a good fisherman never forgets that nature is very strong. It is safer to stay home than to be out in a heavy shower far from land."

That afternoon, a heavy shower did come, just like the report said. Hank smiled and told Mei that checking the weather report every morning helps him protect himself and his boat.

Mei says she wants to become a weather reporter one day, so she can help a fisherman like her father stay safe on the sea.
