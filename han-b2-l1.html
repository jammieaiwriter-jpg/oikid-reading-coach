區塊 1 / 6　① 課文朗讀：The New Team（原創）講義 原創課次對應文本（籃球隊選拔）
原創課次文本，用本課單字和文法寫成（課本課文請看課本跟著念）。一次念一整段，念完做理解題——題目從這篇文字就能作答。
全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Leo is a seventh-grade student. He is not very tall, and his arms and legs are thin. But every day, he works hard on the basketball court. He wants to join the school team this year.

The team has ten players. Coach Wang looks at each player's height. "Leo, you are 165 centimeters tall," she says. "That is not the top height, but it is enough. Do you practice every day?" Leo says yes.

Every morning, Leo practices before class. He does sit-ups and jump shots. He does not like running, but he does it anyway. Hard work makes him strong and fast.

Leo's friend Ken plays guitar and piano, too. Ken does not join the team. He likes music more than sports. "I know I am not a top player," Ken says. "I like to paint and play music instead."

On Saturdays, the team has a big practice. They run, jump, and pass the ball. Some players are large and strong. Others are quick with short legs. Everyone works together.

One player, Tom, has thick, dark hair and a big smile. He makes many three-pointers. "How do you do that?" Leo asks. "I just practice a hundred shots every week," Tom says.

Leo does not give up. He practices his three-pointers every day, too. Slowly, his shots start to go in. His body feels different now — strong arms, a strong body, and more energy.

In the USA, many students play basketball after school, just like Leo's team. Leo smiles and says, "This team is not perfect, but it is my team. I love being part of it!"

Every player on the team has a different body shape. Some are large, and some are thin.

Do you know how to swim? Swimming is a fun sport for the whole team.

Ken plays the guitar and the piano every day after school.

Tom made three baskets, and his last shot was a three-pointer!

Hard work is important. Both boys work hard, but they like different things.

There are one hundred students at the tryouts. That is much more than last year.

Then the coach picks the new players. "You are all winners today," she says.

先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Today is Sports Day at our school. Every student is excited. The sky is blue, and the sun is warm. Students in red, blue, and yellow shirts stand outside on the playground.

Leo is on the blue team. He is not the tallest player, but he is fast. His arm and leg muscles are strong from weeks of practice. He is ready for the race.

The teacher blows a small horn. "Runners, get ready!" she says. Leo and five other students stand in a line. Their hearts beat fast. Everyone in the crowd is quiet for a second.

Go! The runners start to run. Leo's legs move fast. His classmate Ken is in the yellow group, and he shouts, "Go, Leo, go!" The crowd claps and cheers loudly for every runner.

Leo is not first, but he is not last, either. He crosses the line in third place. He is tired, but he smiles a big, proud smile. His team gets more points.

After the race, the students play a fun game with a big rope. Both teams pull hard. The red team and the blue team work together, and they laugh a lot.

At noon, everyone sits down to rest. They drink water and eat lunch outside. "I am tired, but I had a good day," Leo says. "Sports Day is my favorite day of school."

By the end of the day, the blue team has the most points. Leo and his friends jump and clap. "Next year, I want to be first place!" Leo says with a big smile.
