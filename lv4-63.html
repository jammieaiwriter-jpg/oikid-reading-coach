Class Talk Prep 1
How do you spend and save money?
Name one thing you spend on and one way you save.
Reference Answer
I spend money on things I need, and I save some money because I want to buy something important later.
Class Talk Prep 2
Do you have a budget? How much can you save each week?
Use I have or I do not have, then give an amount.
Reference Answer
I have a simple budget, and I can save some money each week for something I want.
Class Talk Prep 3
What should Kane change in his budget, and why?
Say one expense he could reduce and why.
Reference Answer
Kane should spend less money on video games so he can save money for needs and important plans.
