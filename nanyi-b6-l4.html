全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Our class will graduate from junior high next week, and the school gate is decorated with balloons and flowers. Every student checks the bulletin board near the office every morning, because our final exam scores are posted there.

This year, the math exam was hard, and a few students worry that they might fail. Leo is a student who studies every night, but he still feels nervous before he sees his grade.

On Monday, Leo walks to the bulletin board, and his seat number is printed next to his score. His hands are shaking until he finds his name and reads a good grade.

Even though he is happy, Leo also feels a little sad because graduation means saying goodbye to old friends. He looks at the empty seats where his classmates used to sit and feels weird.

Mia, whose hobby is baking, brings homemade cookies to share with the class. She bakes them at home and cuts each cookie so every classmate gets an equal bite.

Mia's mother gives her some pocket money every week, and Mia uses it to buy flour and sugar for baking. She says baking helps her forget about exam stress.

During the graduation ceremony, every student will bow to the teachers to say thank you. Mr. Lin, who has taught our class for three years, tells us that knowledge is more important than grades.

Although the exam is over, our teacher reminds us that learning does not stop after graduation. He says every question we ask helps us find the root of true knowledge, and he will always support us.

區塊 5 / 7　⑤ 文法 C：連接詞 if／(al)though 與介系詞 despite（＝知洞 GP-57）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）

區塊 7 / 7　⑦ GEPT 延伸閱讀：Learning to Ride a Bike講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 200 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Last spring, Ken wanted to learn how to ride a bike. His father found an old bike in the garage and cleaned it for him.

On the first day, Ken sat on the bike, but he could not keep it up. He fell down two times before he even started to move.

His father held the back of the bike and told Ken to look forward, not down. Little by little, Ken began to feel more comfortable on the seat.

Every evening after dinner, Ken and his father practiced in the park near their house. Ken's father ran beside the bike and held on until Ken felt ready to try alone.

One evening, Ken's father let go without telling him. Ken rode the bike by himself for the first time, and he was too excited to notice that his father was not holding on anymore.

Ken shouted with joy and rode around the park two times. His father clapped his hands and said he was very proud of him.

After that day, Ken rode his bike to school every morning. He still remembers how his father taught him to look forward, not down, and never give up.

Now Ken wants to teach his little sister how to ride a bike too. He hopes she will feel as proud as he did on that first evening in the park.
