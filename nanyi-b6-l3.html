全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Our class has started a special project this month. We are writing letters to a sister school in a small village far away. Our teacher says this project is more meaningful than any test we have taken.

It is a great honor to represent our school. Every student has been asked to write at least one sentence about our own family. My sentence is about my great-grandmother, the oldest person in our family.

Our teacher tells us an interesting fact. Long ago, our ancestors were farmers who worked from the countryside to the town center. Their culture is still respected by many people in our village today.

We also talk about festivals. In our culture, we honor our ancestors every spring. In some faraway countries, people celebrate Easter with eggs and baskets instead. Both festivals are important, but they are very different.

Each of us pastes a small stamp onto our letter. The stamp shows the word R.O.C. and a picture of our island. My package is bigger than my friend's package, but hers has more stamps than mine.

On Friday, we walk to the post office. This small business is in the center of town. The worker checks the address, the size of each package, and every small dot on the paper.

By Monday, all of our packages have been sent to the faraway school. They were mailed early in the morning. Now we must wait. Waiting for a letter back feels a little mysterious and exciting.

Three weeks later, a package finally reaches our classroom. Inside, we find photos, a short note, and new words in another language. This is the best guest we have ever welcomed into our class.

區塊 3 / 7　③ 文法 A：現在完成式進階與易錯點（＝知洞 GP-49）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點，複習冊定位：進階應用與易錯點）
現在完成式 have/has + 過去分詞，強調「過去到現在的關聯」。易錯點整理：①already 用在肯定句中間，yet 用在否定句／問句句尾；②for 接一段時間、since 接一個時間點；③句中出現明確過去時間詞（yesterday、last year…）時要改用過去簡單式，不能用現在完成式；④ have been to／have gone to 意思不同；⑤ have/has 要跟主詞一致。規則記好直接作答，答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 4 / 7　④ 文法 B：被動語態進階與易錯點（＝知洞 GP-52）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點，複習冊定位：進階應用與易錯點）
被動語態 be + 過去分詞，強調動作承受者。易錯點整理：①情態助動詞後的被動要用「情態詞 + be + p.p.」（must be finished）；②by + 施動者可以省略，當不知道或不重要時；③問句被動：Was/Were + 主詞 + p.p.？；④現在被動的否定：is/are not + p.p.；⑤未來被動：will be + p.p.。規則記好直接作答，答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 5 / 7　⑤ 文法 C：形容詞副詞最高級進階與易錯點（＝知洞 GP-45）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點，複習冊定位：進階應用與易錯點）
最高級表示「三者以上比較，程度最高」：規則變化加 -est／most，不規則變化要背：good→the best、bad→the worst、far→the farthest/furthest。易錯點整理：①不規則字不可再加 more/most（不能說 more best）；②one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞；③the + 最高級 + in/of + 範圍；④the more..., the more...雙重比較句型；⑤ much/even/a lot 可加強比較級語氣。規則記好直接作答，答完把整句唸出來。

【挑戰閱讀】約 230 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾，遇到沒學過的字用上下文猜，不要停 → ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Twenty years ago, a group of students at Green Hill School did something special. They buried a metal box under the old tree behind the classroom. Inside the box were letters, photos, and small gifts for future students.

Today is the day the box will finally be opened. The whole school has gathered around the tree. Everyone is excited, because no one now at school has ever seen what is inside.

The oldest teacher at school, Mr. Lin, remembers helping bury the box when he was a young student. He says that day felt like the most exciting day of his school life.

The principal digs carefully with a small shovel. After a few minutes, the metal box is found. It has been buried for twenty years, but it still looks in good shape.

The box is opened in front of everyone. Inside, there are old letters written by students who are now adults. There are also photos of the school when it was much smaller than it is today.

One letter says, We hope our school will be even bigger and better in twenty years. Everyone claps, because that wish has come true.

The students decide to start a new time capsule. They will write letters about their own lives and bury a new box under the same tree. It will be opened again in twenty more years.
