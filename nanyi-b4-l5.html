原創課次文本，用本課單字和文法（不定數量代名詞、if/though/despite）寫成（課本課文請看課本跟著念）。一次念一整段，念完做理解題——題目從這篇文字就能作答。
全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Last week, our science class went to the aquarium for a field trip. Our teacher wanted us to learn about nature through real animals, not just books.

Before we went inside, our teacher gave each of us a paper with questions. She said, "Write your answer next to each question after you see the animal."

The first tank had a big shark. Its skin looked smooth from far away, but our guide said it is actually covered with tiny, sharp scales.

Anyone who touched the special model at the front desk could feel how rough a shark's skin really is. Both my friend and I tried it and felt surprised.

Next, we saw a large sea turtle. The guide told us that a turtle does not have a tooth. It uses its hard, sharp beak to eat instead.

Near the turtle tank, there was a sign that said, "Please do not make loud noise here." Some visitors talked quietly, so the turtle did not swim away.

After the tour, we sat outside near a small garden. Our teacher let us plant a small tree there. She said trees change the shape of the land over many years.

On the bus ride home, I looked through my notes and read every answer I wrote. I felt proud that our class could learn so much about nature in one day.

區塊 3 / 8　③ 文法 A-1：不定數量代名詞．基礎（＝知洞 GP-47 上半）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
不定數量代名詞（基礎）：some（一些，肯定句／邀請句）、any（任何，否定句／疑問句）、none（沒有任何一個，＝not any）、all（全部）、both（兩者都）、each（每一個，強調個別）、every（每一個，強調全體，後面接單數名詞）。這些字後面常接 of + 名詞，動詞的單複數要看整體意思判斷。

區塊 4 / 8　④ 文法 A-2：不定數量代名詞．替代用法 one/other（＝知洞 GP-47 下半）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
不定數量代名詞（替代用法）：one/ones 用來代替前面提過的單複數名詞，避免重複（I like this one. / I like the blue ones.）；another 指「再一個（不同的）」；other/others 指「其他的（人或物）」；the other(s) 指「剩下的那個（些）」，通常用在總數確定、已經講了一部分的情況。

區塊 5 / 8　⑤ 文法 B-1：連接詞 if（＝知洞 GP-57 上半）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
連接詞 if：表示「假設、條件」，接在條件子句前，主要子句與條件子句可互換位置（If it rains, we will stay inside. ＝ We will stay inside if it rains.）。談未來的假設，if 子句常用現在式代替未來式。

區塊 6 / 8　⑥ 文法 B-2：(al)though 與介系詞 despite（＝知洞 GP-57 下半）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
連接詞 (al)though 與介系詞 despite：though/although 接完整子句，表示「雖然……（但是）」（Although it was raining, we went out.）；despite 是介系詞，後面直接接名詞或動名詞，不能接完整子句（Despite the rain, we went out. / Despite being tired, she finished.）。although/though 子句和 despite 片語都不能跟 but 同時出現在同一句。

區塊 8 / 8　⑧ GEPT 延伸閱讀：A Visit to the Nature Center講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 210-260 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

My name is Ray. Last Saturday, my whole family went to a nature center near a small river. My little sister was excited because she loves any animal with a strange shape.

At the center, a guide showed us a big turtle. She said the turtle uses its hard beak to eat plants and small fish, because it does not have a tooth like people do.

Next, we walked through a dark tunnel that led to a room full of fish tanks. Some tanks had colorful fish, and others had quiet, gray fish that liked to hide near the rocks.

In one tank, we saw a young shark. Its skin felt rough because of tiny, sharp scales, not smooth like our own skin. My sister touched a model of the skin and made a funny face.

Although the shark looked a little scary, the guide said sharks are not dangerous if people do not bother them. Despite its sharp teeth, this kind of shark mostly eats very small fish.

Near the exit, there was a sign that asked visitors to help nature by not making loud noise and not touching the animals without asking first.

Before we left, my father let each of us plant a small flower in the center's garden. He said, if we come back next year, we can see how much our flowers have grown.

On the way home, I thought about the turtle's beak and the shark's scales. I told myself I want to learn more about nature by myself, not only from books.
