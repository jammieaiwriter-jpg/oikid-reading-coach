A Big Helper at the Convenience Store

短文

Today is Saturday. Leo is at his uncle's convenience store. He wears a blue apron and a big smile. His uncle is the owner, and Leo helps him every week.

The store is small but very busy. The shelves are full of snacks, drinks, and bread. Leo's job is easy. He cleans the shelves and puts new boxes of milk in the fridge.

A young girl comes to Leo. She is thirsty, but her money is only a few coins. Leo counts the coins with her. "You have enough," he says with a kind smile.

Two boys are near the door. They are noisy and a little dirty from soccer practice. One boy's shirt is brown with mud. They are hungry, so they get some fruit and bread.

At three o'clock, an old man comes in. He is Leo's favorite customer. He always brings a picture of his dog. The dog's name is Bo, and Bo is very strong and young.

"This is a new photo," the man says. "Bo gets a prize today. He is the winner of a dog show!" Leo is happy for Bo, and he gives the man a free cookie.

Near closing time, Leo's uncle checks the shelves. "You are a great helper," he says. Leo smiles. He likes this job, and he likes the nice people at the small store.

The Formosan Macaque

短文

Taiwan has many kinds of animals. One special animal is the Formosan macaque. It is a small monkey, and it lives in the mountains and forests of Taiwan.

This monkey is hairy and brown. Its face is pink, and its tail is long. Baby monkeys are cute, but big monkeys are strong.

The Formosan monkey lives in big groups. A group can have twenty or more monkeys. They are not lonely animals; they like to stay with their family.

These monkeys eat fruit, leaves, and small bugs like ants. Sometimes they go to farms and take fruit from the trees. Farmers are not happy about this!

You can see a monkey footprint near a river or a path in the forest. If you see this animal, do not feed it. Wild monkeys are strong, and they can be a little dangerous.

This monkey is a famous animal for people who love nature. It is also a part of Taiwan's forests. This little monkey is a big treasure for the island!

Long ago, hunters hurt many of these monkeys. Now the law protects this animal, so people must not hunt it. Today, more and more monkeys live in the mountains again.

Many mountain paths in Taiwan have signs about this monkey. The signs say, "Please do not feed the monkeys." This rule helps keep both people and monkeys safe and happy.

The Little Lost Dog

短文

It is a sunny afternoon. Mia and her brother Ray are on their way home from school. Near the park, they see a small dog. The dog is brown and a little dirty.

The dog is alone, and it looks scared. It is young, maybe just a few months old. Its ears are big, and its tail is short. The dog is hungry and thirsty, too.

"This is not our dog," Ray says. "But it is so cute!" Mia opens her bag. She has one apple and some bread. She gives the bread to the little dog.

The dog eats fast. Then it is calm and friendly. Mia checks its neck. A small tag is there, but the words are hard to read. The name on the tag is Coco.

"Coco is a nice name," Ray says. He is not angry now, only worried. "Its owner is probably very sad. We are good kids, so we help lost animals."

Mia and Ray walk to the police station near the park. Two officers are friendly and kind. "This dog is not dirty inside, just outside," one officer says with a smile.

Later that day, a woman comes to the station. She is Coco's owner, and she is so happy! "Thank you," she says. "You two are real winners today." Mia and Ray are proud.

短文

Every Saturday, Ivy is a helper at the town library. It's a quiet, wonderful place with many books. Ivy's job is easy but important. She puts books back on the right shelf.

"Where do the cooking books go?" a new helper asks. "They're on the second floor, near the window," Ivy says with a smile. "I remember every shelf here. It's like a map in my head."

"What is your favorite book here?" the boy asks. "It's a book about the USA," Ivy says. "The pictures are beautiful, and the stories are famous. I read it three times already."

An old man comes to the desk. "Who is the author of this book?" he asks Ivy. She checks the cover. "A famous actress writes this one. It's about her family and her life."

At noon, Ivy's father comes to say hello. "Why are you always here on Saturdays?" he asks with a laugh. "Because I love books, and this place feels like home," Ivy says.

Before Ivy leaves, the librarian gives her a small postcard. "You're a wonderful volunteer," she says. Ivy is happy. Her Saturday job is small, but it makes a big difference.

"Please park your car here," Jack tells the first driver. "Then wait on the bench, and we clean your car in fifteen minutes." The man smiles and gives Jack a thumbs up.

"Bring more water, please!" Ella calls to her friend. "Don't forget the soap and the big sponge." Everyone works together, and soon the students are wet from head to toe.

"Let's wash this red car first," Jack says. "Use small circles with the sponge, and don't scratch the paint." His friends listen carefully and work with care.

A woman with two kids stops by. "How much for a big car?" she asks. "It's still 100 dollars," Ella says with a smile. "Please pay at the table after we finish."

"Try this bucket of water," a boy says to a new helper. "Just don't waste the water." They laugh and work fast. The line of cars gets longer and longer.

By the afternoon, the team collects a lot of money. "We did it!" Jack shouts. "Let's buy the new soccer balls tomorrow!" Everyone is tired but very proud of their hard work.

Halloween Fun in Taiwan

短文

Halloween is not a Taiwan holiday, but many people in Taiwan like it now. Every October, schools and shops plan Halloween parties and events.

At school, students often wear a costume for the party. Some students dress like animals. Other students dress like a hero from a movie.

Please bring some candy or a small gift to a Halloween party. It is polite to share with everyone at the party.

At the party, students eat and talk with their friends. They also join games. Some games use a bowl, a spoon, or a small ball.

You do not need to invite too many people to a Halloween party. A small party with your close friends can be very fun, too.

Some families in Taiwan use Halloween as a chance for a family night. They try a new dish, like beef noodles or a special dessert, and just enjoy time together.

Some shops and restaurants also join the fun. A shop may put a pumpkin near the door. A restaurant may put a new dish on the menu just for October.

Halloween in Taiwan is not scary. It is more like a fun new tradition. What a fun holiday it is becoming!

Why Do People Love Pandas?

短文

The panda is one of the most popular animals in the world. Many kids love this black-and-white animal, and zoos are always busy on panda days.

There is a garden area for pandas in many zoos. Inside, there are trees, water, and lots of bamboo for the pandas to eat.

A panda eats bamboo almost all day. It is not a small animal, so it needs a lot of food. Some pandas eat bamboo for twelve hours a day!

Pandas are not really lazy animals, but they do not need to hunt for food like other animals. They just sit near the bamboo and eat.

Between naps, a panda is sometimes hard-working. It climbs trees and plays with other pandas. Baby pandas are small and love to roll on the grass.

People think pandas look kind and gentle. This is one reason so many people love this animal. A panda is also a symbol of love between countries.

There is a panda area near the entrance in many zoos, so it is often the first animal families see. What a lucky animal the panda is!

短文
