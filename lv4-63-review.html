Reference Answer

混合朗讀/口說 (Mixed Reading and Speaking) 2
How much can you save a month?
Say I can save ___ dollars a month.
Reference Answer
I can save one thousand dollars a month when I spend less money on things I do not need.
混合朗讀/口說 (Mixed Reading and Speaking) 3
The coolest bicycle costs forty dollars. If you save five dollars every week, how many weeks do you need?
Say the calculation and answer.
Reference Answer
I need eight weeks because forty dollars divided by five dollars each week equals eight weeks.
