Sustainability Review

Spell key words, read plastic-pollution sentences, and use future tenses

Review Goals
複習頁驗收 plastic pollution 的核心單字、句子、字義配對、future tenses 與 open discussion。
  • 逐格拼出 marine、entangle、medical、disability、wrapper、grocery
  • 能朗讀四個重點句，讀出 plastic pollution 的關鍵意思
  • 能用字義配對完成 review vocabulary，並做 future tense 句型題
  • 能回答如何減少 plastic products 的 open discussion

Grammar Check
Grammar 1
The train ___ at 12:30. (arrive)
未來某個時間正在發生。
Grammar 2
We ___ dinner at a seaside restaurant on Sunday. (have)
未來簡單式。
Grammar 3
It ___ in Brighton tomorrow evening. (snow)
對未來的預測。
Grammar 4
On Friday at 8 o'clock I ___ my friend. (meet)
特定未來時間正在進行。
Grammar 5
This summer, I ___ in Brighton for four years. (live)
到未來某時間已經完成多久。
