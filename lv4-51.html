Paragraph Shadowing
Taipei Districts

Taipei is divided into 12 districts.

A district is an area of a town or city.

It usually has a particular feature, purpose or activity.

The Business District

Xinyi district is an important business district in Taipei.

Many companies, banks and the World Trade Center are located in this district.

Business means making, buying, or selling something for money.

Overpasses and Underpasses

An overpass is a bridge above a road.

Some overpasses help people cross busy streets safely.

Some overpasses are for cars to drive on and to ease traffic.

Underpasses are roads or sidewalks that go under other streets.

People can walk through some underpasses, and these often connect to the metro.

Exploring Taipei

Cindy and Ben had finally arrived in Taiwan.

They were excited to see all of the places in Taipei that John and Cathy had told them about.

The following day, everyone traveled through many busy intersections, underpasses, and overpasses.

It took them only 10 minutes to get to the metro station.

At the Metro and the Zoo

Cindy knew they would take the Blue line to the Brown line first and then walk to the zoo.

The children had a plan in case anyone got lost.

They all would meet at the front entrance of the zoo.

No one got lost, and they had a great day at the zoo.

Class Talk Prep 1
Why is Xinyi district an important business district in Taipei?
說出至少兩個地方，例如 companies 和 banks。
Reference Answer
Xinyi district is important because many companies, banks, and the World Trade Center are there.
Class Talk Prep 2
How can overpasses and underpasses help people in Taipei?
先說 overpass 或 underpass，再說它怎麼幫忙。
Reference Answer
Overpasses help people cross busy streets safely, and underpasses often connect people to the metro.
Class Talk Prep 3
How did the children get to the zoo?
把 taxi、metro、walk 放進一個完整句。
Reference Answer
They took a taxi to the metro station, rode the Blue line to the Brown line, and then walked to the zoo.
