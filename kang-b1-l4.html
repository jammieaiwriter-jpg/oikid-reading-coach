It is early morning. Leo and I are on our way to school. We talk about Safety Week — we are helpers today, and we can't be late!

At the crosswalk, the traffic lights are red. "We can't cross now," I say. The street is noisy, and a car is oncoming, so we wait and listen.

Now the lights are green. "You can cross the road now!" we say to the little kids. "Walk, don't run!"

A boy is walking on the road, not on the sidewalk. "Watch out!" Leo warns him. "Bikes can come fast in this lane. Can you walk on the sidewalk?" "Sure!"

The city has a new app. Every user can download it. It can warn you about noise and busy lanes on your way to school.

Leo and I also design a safety song for Safety Week. "We can make it special!" Leo says. "Can you sing it in a big voice?" "I can!"

Other kids listen to our song at school. It is a real hit! The ground near the gate is safe and clean now. We can all help, big or small.

區塊 3 / 5　③ 文法 A：助動詞 can（＝知洞 GP-14）講義 原創題（對應課本 can 文法點）
肯定：主詞 + can + 原形動詞。否定：can't／cannot + 原形動詞（不加 not can）。疑問：Can + 主詞 + 原形動詞 ~? 回答：Yes, 主詞 + can. / No, 主詞 + can't. can 可以表示『能力』（會、能）也可以表示『請求、允許』（可以嗎、可以），動詞一律用原形，can 後面不加 to。規則記好直接作答，每題答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 5 / 5　⑤ GEPT 延伸閱讀：Grandma's New Computer講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 200 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。短文情境是「教外婆用電腦」，跟課文的才藝表演情境不同。短文裡有幾個還沒教的句型，看懂大意就好，不列入本課文法要求。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

My grandma got a new computer last month. She did not know how to use it, so I helped her every weekend.

First, I showed her how to turn it on and find pictures of our family. She smiled and said, "This is fun!"

Next, we downloaded some music. Grandma likes old songs, so we found her favorite ones. She listened for a long time.

Then I taught her to make a video call. We called my aunt, who lives far away. Grandma's voice sounded so happy on the call.

One day, grandma wanted to read a book on the computer. I showed her a reading program with big letters, so the words were easy to see.

She also learned to write short messages to her friends. At first, she was slow, but she got faster every day.

Now grandma uses the computer every morning. She listens to music, looks at photos, and talks to her family. She says it makes her life more colorful.

I am proud of my grandma. Learning something new is not easy, but she never gives up. I hope I can be like her when I am old.
