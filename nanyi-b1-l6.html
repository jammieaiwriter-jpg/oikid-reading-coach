Every student in Class 701 can show a special skill today. Ben is the first member on stage. He wants to be a super speaker.

Ben can tell a fun story about the sun, the moon, and a rainbow. He can also tell a joke about a fast turtle! Everyone laughs and claps.

Next, Mimi shows her skill. She can sing and dance at the same time. She can even move very fast, and then freeze like ice when the music stops.

Then Jack does a magic trick. He can make a small flash of light. It looks like a star over the mountain and the lake.

The last act is a hero skit. Jack becomes "Captain Sun." He shouts, "Danger! An ice monster wants to attack Earth! It can freeze the river, the ocean, and the sea!"

"Don't worry," says Captain Sun. "I can save the island! I can't let the ice win. The sun can melt the ice, and the water level can rise again."

區塊 3 / 5　③ 文法：助動詞 can 的用法（＝知洞 GP-14）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
can + 動詞原形，表示能力或許可。肯定句：主詞＋can＋原形動詞。否定句：主詞＋can't＋原形動詞。問句：Can＋主詞＋原形動詞？回答 Yes, ... can. / No, ... can't. can 後面一律接原形動詞，不加 s、不變化。直接作答，答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 5 / 5　⑦ GEPT 延伸閱讀：Clean-Up Day at the Park講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】約 220 字原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。 短文情境跟①課文不同（社區公園清掃日），不列入本課文法要求。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Every year, our town has a Clean-Up Day. This year, it is on a Saturday morning in April. My family and I go to the big park near our house to help.

Many people come together. Some are students, and some are parents. We all wear old clothes and bring big bags. Mr. Lee, the park leader, gives us a job. My job is to pick up trash near the lake.

My little brother wants to help too, but he is not very strong. He can carry small things, like empty bottles and cans. We put glass bottles in one bag and cans in another bag. It is easy when we work together.

After one hour, our bags are very full and heavy. My father helps us carry them to a big truck. Then we drink some cold water and rest under a tree.

Next, we plant new flowers near the path. My mother waters them with a can of water. The flowers will grow tall and colorful in a few weeks. This makes the park more beautiful for everyone.

By noon, the park looks great! There is no trash on the grass or near the lake. Mr. Lee thanks all of us and smiles.

"You did a wonderful job today," he says. We are tired, but we feel proud. We will come back next year!

My little brother wants to help too, but he is not very strong. He can carry small things, like empty bottles and cans. We put glass bottles in one bag and cans in another bag. It is easy when we work together.
唸 4
After one hour, our bags are very full and heavy. My father helps us carry them to a big truck. Then we drink some cold water and rest under a tree.
唸 5
Next, we plant new flowers near the path. My mother waters them with a can of water. The flowers will grow tall and colorful in a few weeks. This makes the park more beautiful for everyone.
唸 6
