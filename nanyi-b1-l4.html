區塊 6 / 6　⑦ GEPT 延伸閱讀：Nina's Birthday Treasure Hunt講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程：①先整篇從頭讀到尾——遇到沒學過的字用上下文猜，不要停 → ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。情境是生日派對尋寶遊戲，與①課文（新家房間導覽）不同。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Today is my cousin Nina's birthday. Her party is at the park near her house. It is a warm, sunny day, and everyone is excited.

There are ten children at the party. There is a big yellow table under a tree. On the table, there are balloons, cups, and a beautiful cake. The cake looks like a panda!

"Where is the birthday girl?" Nina's mom asks. "She is behind the tree with her friends," someone says. We all shout, "Surprise!" when Nina comes out. She is so happy.

After lunch, we play a fun game called a treasure hunt. Nina's uncle hides small gifts around the park. "What do we need to find?" a boy asks. "You need to find five yellow boxes," Nina's uncle says.

We make two teams. My team is hard-working, and we run fast. "Who can find a box first?" I ask my friend.
