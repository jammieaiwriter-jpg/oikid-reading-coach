全文（點任一字查中文意思）

Wei-Lin's class got a new classmate this month. His name was Daniel, and he came from Canada as an exchange student. He was a shy teenager who spoke very little Chinese at first.

On his first day, Daniel sat next to Wei-Lin. He looked afraid when the teacher wrote new vocabulary on the blackboard with a piece of chalk. Wei-Lin passed him a notebook and a small dictionary.

During the English class, the teacher gave a short quiz about last week's subject. Daniel didn't understand every question, so Wei-Lin helped explain each one quietly. Daniel was thankful for such a helpful classmate.

After class, Wei-Lin opened her desk drawer and gave Daniel a marker, some tape, and a bottle of glue for his art workbook. "Careful, this glue is sticky," she said with a smile.

During lunch, Daniel felt stupid because he didn't know where the gym or the copy machine was. Wei-Lin walked with him and showed him the whole building, except the teachers' office.

That afternoon, they hurried to the playground before break ended. Daniel said he probably needed a short nap after the long quiz, but he still wanted to play a game first.

On the weekend, Wei-Lin took Daniel to a small bookstore near a church, and then to the post office to mail an envelope to his family. He wanted to send them a letter about his new school.

That night, Daniel wrote in his diary about the classmate whose kindness made his first week easier. He decided to pack a small gift for Wei-Lin the next week to say thank you.

區塊 3 / 6　③ 文法 A：所有格關係代名詞 whose（＝知洞 GP-56 進階面向 A）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
GP-56 進階面向：whose 是所有格關係代名詞，代替「代名詞所有格＋名詞」，可指人也可指物，後面一定接名詞（whose + 名詞）。例：Daniel is the student. His backpack is full of books. → Daniel is the student whose backpack is full of books. 這區不出 who/which/that 主格、受格題型（留給 L5），只練 whose。

區塊 4 / 6　⑤ 文法 B：地方／時間關係副詞 where、when（＝知洞 GP-56 進階面向 B）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點）
GP-56 進階面向：where（＝in/at/on which）修飾地方名詞、when（＝in/at/on which）修飾時間名詞，後面接完整子句，不缺主詞或受詞。例：This is the bookstore. I bought my dictionary there. → This is the bookstore where I bought my dictionary. 這區不出 who/which/that 主格、受格題型（留給 L5），只練 where/when。

先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

My name is Mia. Last month, a new student named Leo joined my class. He moved to our town because his parents changed jobs, and he did not know anyone here at first.

On his first day, Leo looked nervous. He sat alone at lunch and did not talk to anyone. I decided to sit next to him and ask about his old school.

Leo told me his old classroom was near a small park, and his best friend lived right next door. He said he missed that friend a lot.

I wanted to help Leo make new friends, so I invited him to join our lunch group. At first, he was quiet, but he slowly started to smile more.

A week later, our teacher planned a class trip to a museum. Leo asked me many questions about the bus, what to bring, and when to come.

During the trip, Leo talked with more classmates than before. He even helped a boy who dropped his lunch box. Everyone thanked him for being so kind.

After the trip, Leo said he finally felt like part of the class. He thanked me for being the first person who talked to him.

Now Leo has many friends, and he even helps new students feel welcome, just like I once helped him.
