區塊 1 / 7　① 課文朗讀：The Giant Bee Headline（原創）講義 原創課次對應文本（辨識網路假新聞）
原創課次文本，用本課單字寫成（課本課文請看課本跟著念）。一次念一整段，念完做理解題——題目從這篇文字就能作答。
全文（點任一字查中文意思）

One lazy Sunday, Leo sat on a bench in his yard and looked at his phone. A big headline said, 'Giant Bees Attack the City Park!' The photo even showed dark smoke behind the giant bee, and the words looked scary.

While Leo was reading, his neighbor Mr. Chen walked by and saw his face. 'What is wrong?' asked Mr. Chen. Leo showed him the post. Mr. Chen laughed and said, 'That headline sounds over-the-top. Let's spot the truth together.'

Mr. Chen has a sharp eye for these tricks. He asked Leo to check the source of the post. It came from a strange website, not a trusted news page that people know.

They looked at the photo again. The bee looked giant because of a strange camera angle, not because it was really that big. It was just a normal insect, but someone made a copy of an old photo and changed the size.

The photo was actually taken far away, near an old bridge in another country. Someone made an error and said it happened in their own city park. That was the biggest clue that the story was fake.

The next day, Leo's teacher used the story in class. She picked up a piece of chalk and wrote three tips on the board: check the source, find the true photo, and ask if the news feels fake.

Leo's teacher also told a true fact: bees do not attack people for no reason, and they make honey, not fear. Bees are helpful insects, just like ants, and most small bugs in a yard are not dangerous at all.

Later, Leo found the true story. A foreigner in China took the first photo after a small typhoon blew leaves near a bridge. That photo was real, but the giant bee headline about Leo's city was completely fake.

區塊 3 / 7　③ 文法 A：從屬連接詞 when/while/before/as soon as/until（＝知洞 GP-33，複習冊：進階應用與易錯點）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點，複習冊定位）
複習重點：while 強調「正在進行」的背景動作，when 強調「那一刻」發生的事；until 常跟 not 連用（not ... until，表示「直到才」）；as soon as／時間副詞子句要用現在式代表未來，不能用 will；before 單純講先後順序。規則記好直接作答，每題答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 4 / 7　④ 文法 B：連接詞 if 和 (al)though 與介系詞 despite（＝知洞 GP-57，複習冊：進階應用與易錯點）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點，複習冊定位）
複習重點：although／though 後面接一個完整子句（有主詞、有動詞）；despite 是介系詞，後面只能接名詞或動名詞（despite the rain / despite raining），不能直接接完整子句（despite it rained 是常見錯誤）；if 用來講條件。規則記好直接作答，答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 5 / 7　⑤ 文法 C：why 問句與從屬連接詞 because 與 so（＝知洞 GP-34，複習冊：進階應用與易錯點）講義 原創題（對應課本文法點，複習冊定位）
複習重點：why 問句問原因；because 帶出「原因」子句，so 帶出「結果」子句——順序是 [原因] , so [結果]，或 Because [原因], [結果]，兩個字不能在同一句裡疊用（because...so... 是常見錯誤）。規則記好直接作答，答完把整句唸出來。

區塊 7 / 7　⑦ GEPT 延伸閱讀：The Old Bookstore講義 原創短文（GEPT 初級字彙範圍）
【挑戰閱讀】原創短文，字彙在英檢初級 2000 字內。流程刻意這樣排：①直接把整篇文章從頭讀到尾——會遇到幾個沒學過的字，用上下文猜，不要停（考試就是這樣）→ ②做理解題驗證有沒有讀懂（答案都在文中，用讀的找，不要猜選項）→ ③生字補充，確認剛才猜的意思 → ④最後一段一段朗讀收尾。
先把整篇文章讀完（用眼睛讀，還不用唸）

Last weekend, my father and I visited an old bookstore near our home. The store is small, but it has thousands of books. Some books are new, and some are secondhand.

The owner is an old man named Mr. Wu. He has worked at the store for thirty years, and he knows almost every book on every shelf in the shop.

My father wanted to find a cooking book. Mr. Wu walked to a tall shelf and picked one out in a few seconds. The book was old, but its pages were still clean.

I looked around the store too. I found a comic book with a small price tag. It only cost twenty dollars, so I bought it right away and felt very happy.

Mr. Wu told us a story about the shop. Many years ago, his father started this small business. Now Mr. Wu runs it every day with his own son.

He said the store is like a treasure box. Every book has its own story, and every visitor finds something a little different to enjoy.

Before we left, Mr. Wu gave me a free bookmark. He smiled and said, 'Come back soon, and bring your friends with you next time.'

On the way home, I read the first page of my new comic book. I already love that old bookstore, and I want to visit it again next month.
